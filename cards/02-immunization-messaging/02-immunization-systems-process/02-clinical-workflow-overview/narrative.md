The following outlines common steps in clinical workflows related to immunization data exchange:

**Patient Registration**: Patient demographic data is collected or confirmed prior to or during an encounter. This process is often distinct from immunization data entry and may occur at a different time, location, or by different personnel.

**Appointment Preparation**: Submitting/querying systems may query the IIS for the patient's immunization history and forecast to support decision-making. Queries may occur well before the encounter or at the time of service.

**Historical Immunizations**: Immunizations documented on paper or received elsewhere may be entered into the system during the encounter and included in submissions to the IIS.

**Vaccination Administration**: New vaccinations are administered and recorded. These may be private or publicly funded. Jurisdictional policies govern which vaccinations must be reported to the IIS, with public vaccine programs typically requiring reporting within a working day.

**Post-Administration Guidance**: Forecasts for future doses may be generated by the submitting system or retrieved from the IIS via query. Patients may be informed of next steps using this guidance.

**Data Transmission to IIS**:

Updates: Submitting systems send VXU messages to report administered or historical vaccinations, and to revise or delete previous submissions.

Queries: QBP messages request patient immunization records. Queries may be forecast-optional or forecast-required. The IIS response includes immunization history and, if applicable, recommendations for future doses.

Immunization messages rely on standardized code sets to ensure consistent meaning across systems:

**CVX**: CDC-maintained codes for vaccine types, used to represent a patient's lifetime immunization history.

**NDC**: FDA-maintained product codes for vaccines and other drugs.

**MVX**: Manufacturer codes for vaccines.

**LOINC & SNOMED CT**: Used in observation (OBX) segments to convey clinical information such as immunity status or contraindications.

Maintaining current and accurate vocabulary tables is critical for ensuring valid message content and supporting cross-jurisdictional interoperability.

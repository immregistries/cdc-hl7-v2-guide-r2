The HL7 v2.5.1 Implementation Guide for Immunization Messaging, Release 2.0 defines how Immunization Information Systems (IIS) exchange data with submitting/querying systems, a category that includes Electronic Health Records (EHRs), pharmacy systems, school health systems, and other health IT platforms that either report or request immunization data.

This guide is based on HL7 Version 2.5.1, a widely adopted standard for healthcare messaging. HL7 v2 was originally developed in the 1980s and remains in use because it is compact, mature, well-supported, and highly interoperable. While more modern standards like HL7 FHIR exist today, HL7 v2 remains the most commonly deployed standard for immunization messaging due to its long history of implementation in public health systems.

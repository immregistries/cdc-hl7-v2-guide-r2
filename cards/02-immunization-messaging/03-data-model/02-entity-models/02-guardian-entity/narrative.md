The Guardian entity represents an adult responsible for a patient's care, most commonly a mother, father, or legal guardian. This concept is closely related to what is referred to elsewhere in this guide as a responsible person, the role conveyed through NK1 segment messaging (see Key Terms). The two terms differ slightly in that Guardian consolidates the "responsible person" and "mother's name" concepts defined separately by the CDC Core Data Elements into a single entity. A mother reported for matching purposes and any other adult responsible for the patient's care serve the same functional role in this data model, identifying an adult connected to the patient who can support record matching.

- name (last, first, middle)
- relationshipToPatient (mother/father/guardian)

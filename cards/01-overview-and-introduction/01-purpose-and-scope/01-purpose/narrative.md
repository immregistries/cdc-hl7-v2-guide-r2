The purpose of this Implementation Guide (IG) is to improve how immunization data are shared between systems such as Immunization Information Systems (IIS) and Electronic Health Records (EHRs). It defines a consistent, standards-based framework for exchanging vaccination data using HL7 Version 2.5.1 messages, enabling better communication, reducing data-exchange errors, and supporting accurate, complete immunization histories across the health ecosystem.

This guide is developed for use within the United States. Unless otherwise specified, references to "national" standards, policy, or authorities throughout this guide refer to the United States federal level, as distinct from state, local, or jurisdictional levels.

This guide provides the technical foundation for creating, sending, receiving, and interpreting electronic immunization messages. It builds upon prior HL7 v2.5.1 and v2.8.2 immunization messaging guides and incorporates lessons learned from decades of implementation and community feedback. It has been developed through collaboration among IIS programs, EHR developers, public health agencies, and other experts dedicated to ensuring that clinical and public health systems work together to protect individuals and communities from vaccine-preventable diseases.

In support of this purpose, this guide defines requirements across several interrelated areas: message transport and connectivity, patient and organization identification, data standardization for key clinical and administrative elements, clinical decision support content returned in query responses, and the acknowledgement model used to communicate processing outcomes to submitting systems. A central concept introduced in this guide is core intent, the determination of whether a submitted patient and vaccination history were successfully associated with and recorded in the IIS, which governs how acceptance and rejection are communicated. See Acknowledgement (ACK) Message Profile for the complete interpretation model, and see Messaging Profiles and Transactions and Data Standardization and Integration for the detailed requirements in these areas.

This guide also documents the national compliance context for immunization messaging, including its relationship to ONC certification criteria, and establishes how local jurisdictions should represent their own implementation guides relative to this national standard. See National Compliance and ONC Regulations for details.

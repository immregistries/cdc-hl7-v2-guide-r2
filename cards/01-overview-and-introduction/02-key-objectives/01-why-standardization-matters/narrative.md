In the early years of IIS development, data were often entered manually or exchanged using custom flat-file formats, some literally carried by floppy disk. The community adopted HL7 v2 not because it was technically superior, but because it provided a common standard that everyone, public health programs, vendors, and clinical partners, could agree to use. A shared standard reduces complexity, integration costs, and maintenance burdens across the dozens of IIS and hundreds of connected systems nationwide.

Adherence to this IG promotes alignment, not rigidity. IIS are encouraged to follow the national standard wherever possible, not for the sake of compliance itself, but to simplify onboarding, strengthen data quality, and enable collective improvement across jurisdictions.

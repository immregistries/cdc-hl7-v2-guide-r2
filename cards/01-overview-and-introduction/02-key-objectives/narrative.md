The objective of this Implementation Guide (IG) is not simply to mandate the use of HL7 Version 2 messaging, but to help Immunization Information Systems (IIS) and their partners exchange accurate, high-quality immunization data efficiently and consistently.

HL7 is the means-not the mission. The mission of every IIS is to receive, consolidate, and provide complete and reliable immunization histories for clinical and public-health use. The HL7 v2 standard serves this mission by providing a shared language that reduces technical friction and allows diverse systems to communicate effectively.

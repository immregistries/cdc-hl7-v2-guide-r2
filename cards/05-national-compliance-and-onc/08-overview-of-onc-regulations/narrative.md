Federal health IT regulations have historically played a significant role in shaping expectations for immunization data exchange, particularly through the Health IT Certification Program administered by the Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology (ONC). Prior certification criteria established baseline immunization functionality for submitting systems and informed the evolution of this implementation guide.

At the time of publication, the regulatory landscape governing certification and public health interoperability is evolving, and future requirements affecting immunization messaging, IIS, and submitting systems may change. This guide is intentionally written to remain technically stable and applicable across regulatory transitions by focusing on clearly defined messaging behavior and conformance expectations, independent of specific certification mandates.

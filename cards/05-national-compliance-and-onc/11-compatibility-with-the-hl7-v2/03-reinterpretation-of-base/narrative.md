A number of fields have taken on new, domain-specific meaning within IIS implementations:

RXA-9 now indicates administered vs historical vaccinations, or vaccine event record type, becoming a critical driver of business logic.

RXA-11 is consistently used to identify the administering organization rather than a physical bed or room location.

These reinterpretations are mature, universal, and non-negotiable. Aligning them back to the original semantics is neither feasible nor beneficial.

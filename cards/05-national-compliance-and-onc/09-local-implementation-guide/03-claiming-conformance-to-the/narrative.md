Claiming conformance to the National IG is a representation to partners and the broader community, and it carries expectations of reusability and alignment with nationally agreed behavior. Jurisdictions making such claims must ensure that consumers can discover how the claim was determined and where to find any local differences.

  - "Conformant with the National IG": used when the Local IG is compliant with National IG requirements (differences, if any, are constraints that preserve compliance).

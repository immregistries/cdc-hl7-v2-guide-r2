Local implementation guides (IGs) are derived from the National IG and may introduce additional constraints to support local policy or operational needs. Deviation from the National IG is not inherently problematic; the concern is lack of clarity about when a deviation exists.

The National IG defines the technical interoperability semantics for immunization messaging. Jurisdictions retain authority to implement local policy, but they must not describe behavior that contradicts the National IG as conformant; any deviations from the National IG must be clearly documented.

Certification demonstrates readiness to participate in interoperable exchange; it does not represent completion. Systems evolve, standards advance, and operational realities change. This guide is intended to support continuous improvement by providing a shared technical reference for evaluating system behavior over time.

By applying this guide across certification, onboarding, and live operations, IIS and their partners can align technical conformance with public health objectives while accommodating real-world variability inherent in immunization data exchange.

The 2015 Edition Health IT Certification Criteria established required immunization capabilities for certified EHR technology, including the ability to record, display, submit, and query immunization data using prior releases of this implementation guide. These criteria are evaluated using standardized test procedures historically developed and published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). This guide is intended to provide the technical specification needed to support such certification criteria; it does not itself define certification policy or enumerate which capabilities ONC will require.

Certification testing focuses on whether systems can generate and process messages that meet defined requirements under controlled scenarios. It does not assess jurisdiction-specific policy, operational workflows, or long-term data quality outcomes.

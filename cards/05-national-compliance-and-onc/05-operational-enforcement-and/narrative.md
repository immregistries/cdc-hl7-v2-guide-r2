Conformance to this guide is assessed across multiple stages: certification testing, onboarding, and live operations. In production, IIS play a central role in monitoring data quality trends and identifying systemic gaps that indicate non-conformant behavior.

Evaluation of compliance should focus on sustained patterns rather than isolated message failures. For example, persistent absence of RE data elements across submissions may indicate a system capability gap rather than missing source data.

Submitting systems that are unable to meet the functional expectations established through certification testing and demonstrated through sustained productions performance SHALL NOT claim conformance to this guide.

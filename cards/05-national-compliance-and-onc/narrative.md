This implementation guide supports national interoperability objectives established through the federal Health IT Certification Program, including the 2015 Edition and subsequent HTI-1 and HTI-2 regulations. These efforts are led by the Office of the National Coordinator for Health IT (ONC), which defines certification criteria to ensure that health IT systems support interoperable data exchange and public health use cases.

Historically, certification requirements have focused on submitting systems, particularly EHRs. More recent policy direction signals an expansion of expectations for public health infrastructure, including Immunization Information Systems (IIS). This guide provides the technical foundation needed to support certification of immunization messaging functionality across both submitting and receiving systems.

It is critical to distinguish between certification criteria and certification mandates. ONC defines what it means for a system to be certifiable but does not require any specific system to be certified. Requirements to use certified systems are established independently through federal programs, state policy, funding conditions, or contractual agreements. Similarly, this guide does not require systems to implement specific functionality; it defines what systems must do if they claim conformance to the guide.

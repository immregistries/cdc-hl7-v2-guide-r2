The VXU message profile (Z22 - Update, Unsolicited Immunization Update) supports the submission of immunization-related events from a submitting system to an Immunization Information System (IIS). It is the primary mechanism by which administered and historical vaccinations, along with limited related updates, are reported to the IIS to maintain a complete, consolidated immunization history for a patient.

VXU messaging is event-driven. Messages are created in response to changes in immunization records, most commonly the administration or documentation of a vaccination, but also corrections, deletions, or the addition of historically known doses. While VXU messages can carry some non-vaccination data, they are operationally anchored to vaccination events and are not intended to serve as a general-purpose demographic or public health data transport.

Failure to align VXU usage with these event-driven expectations leads to inconsistent reporting behavior, overloading of IIS with inappropriate updates, and misuse of immunization workflows to transmit unrelated data. This section defines when VXU messages are expected to be created and how they SHALL be used by submitting systems.

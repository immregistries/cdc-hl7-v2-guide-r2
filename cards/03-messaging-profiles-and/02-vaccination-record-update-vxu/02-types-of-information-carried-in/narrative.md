Limited patient demographic information

The presence of immunization content determines whether a VXU is an appropriate vehicle for transmission.

VXU messages are primarily intended for immunization-related exchanges. However, they may also accommodate demographic-only submissions under specific circumstances, such as correcting a patient's name, address, or date of birth on an existing immunization record, or updating demographic information in advance of an anticipated vaccination event.

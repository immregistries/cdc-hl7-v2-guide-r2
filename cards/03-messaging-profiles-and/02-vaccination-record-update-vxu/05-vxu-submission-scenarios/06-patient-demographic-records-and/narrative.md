Patient demographic information and immunization documentation are often managed through separate workflows in submitting systems. In contrast, IIS maintain a unified patient record in which demographic and immunization information are closely linked and jointly used to support functions such as forecasting, deduplication, coverage assessment, and patient matching. As a result, demographic updates may be operationally relevant to IIS even when no new immunization information is being reported.

Submitting systems MAY send VXU messages containing patient demographic information with or without associated immunization events. There is no general requirement that demographic changes be reported to the IIS whenever they occur, and this guide does not establish VXU as a comprehensive mechanism for synchronizing patient demographics. Instead, VXU demographic updates are intended to support immunization-related workflows where the IIS requires sufficient patient context to maintain an accurate and usable record.

Historically, some IIS have restricted acceptance of VXUs that do not contain immunization events, particularly to avoid creating patient records without documented vaccinations. While these policies reflected earlier operational constraints, IIS today function as population-wide, lifetime systems that support forecasting, tracking, and reporting for all patients, including those who have not received any vaccinations. In these environments, rejecting demographic-only VXUs at the interface level limits data quality, delays record establishment, and prevents submitters from engaging with IIS functionality that depends on the existence of a patient record.

For these reasons, IIS SHALL accept VXU messages that contain patient demographic information regardless of whether immunization events are included. Submitting systems SHALL coordinate with the IIS prior to sending demographic-only VXUs on an ongoing or routine basis, so that the IIS can confirm its capacity to receive and process this data appropriately. IIS that wish to exclude patients without immunizations from specific calculations, reports, or denominators SHOULD do so within IIS processing and analytics, rather than by rejecting submitted data. Acceptance of demographic-only VXUs does not imply that such submissions are required or expected in all cases. This guide's acceptance requirement establishes a baseline; individual IIS may maintain additional, locally defined guidelines regarding acceptable demographic data based on their specific operational needs and practices. Detailed guidance on demographic data quality and acceptance criteria is provided in the sections that follow (see section 4.5, Patient Information).

Submitting systems SHOULD limit demographic-only VXUs to situations that support immunization-related workflows and SHOULD use more appropriate HL7 v2 message types (e.g., ADT) for ongoing or routine demographic and auxiliary data updates when available. The decision to submit demographic information via VXU remains an implementation choice, except in the specific scenarios defined in the following sections where VXU submission is required.

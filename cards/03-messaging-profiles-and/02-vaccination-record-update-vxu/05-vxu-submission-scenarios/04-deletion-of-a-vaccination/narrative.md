Submitting systems must be able to correct errors by removing vaccination records they previously reported. In practice, incorrect vaccinations may be submitted due to documentation errors, data entry mistakes, or later discovery that a reported vaccination did not occur. Allowing submitters to request deletion of their own records is essential to maintaining data quality and trust in the IIS.

Vaccinations SHALL NOT be reported to the IIS until they are confirmed to be completed and recorded as final. Reporting anticipated or planned vaccinations, followed by later deletion, is not an appropriate use of VXU delete functionality and creates unnecessary churn and ambiguity in IIS records. Delete messages are intended to support correction of mistakes, not to serve as a routine mechanism for managing provisional or incomplete vaccination data.

Because IIS consolidate immunization information from multiple sources, the effect of a delete request may vary depending on the broader record context. In some cases, a vaccination reported by one organization may correspond to an administered dose reported by another organization or be otherwise supported by independent evidence already present in the IIS. In these situations, an IIS may retain a vaccination record despite receiving a delete request, based on its internal evaluation and consolidation rules.

However, IIS must support deletion requests in the straightforward case where a vaccination record was reported only by the submitting organization and is known to be incorrect. Failing to support deletes in these situations causes erroneous data to persist indefinitely and undermines the ability of submitters and IIS to collaboratively maintain accurate immunization histories. For this reason, IIS are required to support VXU delete messages from submitting systems for vaccination records they originally submitted, even though the final disposition of a delete may depend on the specific data context.

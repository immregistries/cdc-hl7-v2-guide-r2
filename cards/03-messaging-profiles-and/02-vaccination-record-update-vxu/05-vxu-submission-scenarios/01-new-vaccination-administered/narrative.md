When one or more vaccinations are administered and recorded during a patient encounter, the submitting system is expected to notify the IIS of those events. Reporting is generally expected to occur on the day the vaccination is administered to support timely availability of immunization information for clinical care, forecasting, and public health use.

VXU submissions SHALL NOT occur until the vaccination has been completed and recorded as final in the submitting system. Intermediate or provisional states, such as ordered, planned, or partially documented vaccinations, do not represent completed immunization events and should not be reported. This does not preclude reporting of other clinically relevant statuses, such as NA (Not Administered) or PA (Partial Administration), which represent finalized outcomes of a vaccination attempt rather than provisional or in-progress documentation. Once the vaccination event is finalized, it should be transmitted in a manner consistent with the submitting system's operational workflow and the guidance in this section.

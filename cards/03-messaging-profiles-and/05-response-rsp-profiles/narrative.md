A response is generated after the IIS has received a query containing all patient identifying information known to the querying system, including demographic attributes and available patient identifiers. The IIS applies its internal matching processes to this information in order to identify the patient record-or set of records-that best corresponds to the query.

The intended outcome of a query is a single, high-confidence match. In many cases, particularly when the querying system also submits vaccination updates to the IIS, the patient record already exists and can be identified quickly using previously established identifiers. These situations allow the IIS to return a complete immunization history, with or without evaluation and forecast, in a single response.

In other situations, the IIS may be unable to identify a single definitive match. The patient may be new to the IIS, identifying information may be incomplete or inconsistent, or multiple records may meet the matching criteria. Depending on policy and technical capability, an IIS may either stop at this point-indicating that a definitive match cannot be returned-or provide limited identifying information for one or more possible matches so that a querying system can assist in selecting the appropriate record.

Response profiles define the controlled set of outcomes for these situations. They specify how an IIS conveys a successful match, presents candidate matches, or indicates that a record cannot be returned. By constraining response behavior to a small number of well-defined patterns, RSP profiles enable querying systems to interpret results safely, respect patient privacy, and respond appropriately to both successful and ambiguous matching outcomes.

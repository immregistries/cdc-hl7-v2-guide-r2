Accurate patient matching is a shared responsibility between the querying system and the IIS. Querying systems are responsible for providing complete and current identifying information, while IIS are responsible for applying appropriate matching logic to determine the most likely patient record or set of candidate records.

Effective matching depends on cooperation between systems over time. Information previously submitted to the IIS, particularly patient identifiers, can significantly improve match accuracy and performance when reused consistently in subsequent queries.

Querying System Responsibilities

Patient identifiers such as Medical Record Numbers (MRNs) SHALL be included when available.

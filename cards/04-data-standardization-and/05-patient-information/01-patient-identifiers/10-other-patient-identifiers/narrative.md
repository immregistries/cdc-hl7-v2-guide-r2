A jurisdiction may permit additional identifiers in PID-3 when they support a defined operational or matching purpose. These identifiers can carry different privacy, security, and data-quality risks and are governed by jurisdictional policy and the onboarding agreement.

An additional identifier does not create a general requirement for the IIS to retain it, use it in patient matching, or return it in an RSP. The minimum national expectation is limited to the submitter's primary exchange identifier and the IIS registry identifier.

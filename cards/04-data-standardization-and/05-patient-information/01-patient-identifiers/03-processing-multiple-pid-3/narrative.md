PID-3 can contain multiple patient identifiers. Repetition order does not determine the meaning or priority of an identifier. Systems identify the relevant value by examining the identifier type rather than assuming that a particular identifier appears first.

An IIS may receive the submitter's primary exchange identifier, an IIS registry identifier, and other identifiers in the same message. The IIS processes the identifier types it supports and may ignore unsupported identifiers according to jurisdictional policy.

Consent models differ between jurisdictions:

Opt-out: All patient records are sent to the IIS unless the patient explicitly opts out.

Opt-in: Patient records are not accepted unless the patient has explicitly opted in.

Mandatory participation: Some jurisdictions require inclusion of all patients, regardless of consent.

In opt-out jurisdictions, submitters typically send all records. IIS systems, not senders, manage opt-out enforcement. In opt-in jurisdictions, records may be rejected unless consent is documented.

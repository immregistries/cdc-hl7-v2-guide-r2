Accurate representation of names for other people associated with the vaccination, parents, guardians, and providers, supports auditing, accountability, and linkage of immunization events to responsible organizations or individuals.

Applicable fields:

- PID-6/QPD-5 Mother's Maiden Name
- NK1-2 Responsible Person (Guardian) Name
- ORC-1 Entered By
- ORC-12 Ordering Provider
- RXA-10 Administering Provider

Same formatting principles used for patient names apply here. These names must be transmitted consistently and with correct classification. Minimum supported length for name components is typically defined by the IIS and therefore varies; this guide does not set a universal lower bound, though guidance on minimum lengths may be added in a future version of the IG following discussion with the community.

- The previous requirement that 'L' appear in the first repetition has been retired; order is not enforced.

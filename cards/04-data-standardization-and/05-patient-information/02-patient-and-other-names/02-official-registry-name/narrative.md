The Official Registry Name corresponds to what HL7 previously called the legal name. The term "official registry" emphasizes that the name should match as closely as possible the one found on official documents, such as a birth certificate, driver's license, or passport. This is typically the most stable and comparable name across systems.

The official registry name is generally required for establishing a record in an IIS. Submitters should still report records even if the official registry name is unknown; the IIS will decide whether to accept them based on local policy.

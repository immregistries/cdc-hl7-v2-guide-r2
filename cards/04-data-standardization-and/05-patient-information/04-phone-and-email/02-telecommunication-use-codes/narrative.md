Telecommunication use codes were originally intended to describe the context in which a contact method was used (e.g., residence, work, vacation). In current IIS practice, their practical purpose is limited to distinguishing phone numbers from email addresses.

Only two use codes are required for basic interoperability:

PRN (Primary Residence Number) - designates the primary phone number for contacting the patient or guardian.

NET (Network Email Address) - designates an email address.

Other use codes may be sent when collected by the submitting system but are not required for IIS operations.

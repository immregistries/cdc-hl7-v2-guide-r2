| **Code** | **Maintained by** | **Primary purpose** | **Notes** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CVX**<br>Vaccine Administered Code | CDC | Represents which vaccine was given; canonical vocabulary for IIS immunization history, evaluation, and forecasting | Preferred standard |
| **NDC**<br>National Drug Code | FDA | Identifies specific manufactured vaccine product and packaging; used for inventory, lot tracking, and traceability | Product-Level Detail |
| **CPT**<br>Current Procedural Terminology | American Medical Association (AMA) | Billing/procedure codes historically used to report vaccination activity | Discouraged |

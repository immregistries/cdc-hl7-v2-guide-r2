Vaccines may be associated with multiple NDCs depending on how the product is packaged and administered:

Unit-of-Sale (UoS): Identifies the packaged product as distributed (e.g., multi-dose vial or package).

Unit-of-Use (UoU): Identifies the product at the point of administration.

Both UoU and UoS NDCs may appear in immunization messaging, and IIS are expected to recognize and interpret either form in the context of administered vaccinations.

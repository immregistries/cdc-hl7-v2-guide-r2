Unspecified Formulation or category-level CVX codes are appropriate for historical vaccinations when the exact formulation or product cannot reasonably be determined. They are also appropriate when the code represents the most specific CVX available for that vaccine concept, and for representing foreign vaccinations when no more specific CVX code exists.

Use of Unspecified Formulation and Category-Level CVX Codes for Administered Vaccinations

Use of a broader CVX code is preferable to omitting the vaccination entirely when specific product information is unavailable.

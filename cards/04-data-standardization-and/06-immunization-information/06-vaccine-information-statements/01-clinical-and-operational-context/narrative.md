VIS requirements apply at the clinical encounter. In busy care environments, families may receive multiple VIS documents at once, often bundled with other paperwork. EHR systems typically manage VIS data by maintaining internal tables linking CVX codes to their current VIS identifiers and dates. As a result, the VIS information transmitted to an IIS generally reflects the EHR's configuration rather than the real-time workflow at the encounter, and IIS should not interpret the presence or absence of VIS data as evidence of VICP compliance.

IIS SHOULD scope any VIS-related data-quality efforts to clearly defined program purposes. For example, a state or local public health authority may rely on its IIS to gather VIS metadata, such as which VIS edition was associated with an administered vaccine and its publication date, in order to check whether providers are following VIS distribution practices required by law or funding programs.

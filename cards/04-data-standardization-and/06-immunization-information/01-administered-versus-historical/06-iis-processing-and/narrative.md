IIS use the administered/historical distinction internally to support reconciliation and deduplication. Reconciliation is commonly performed at the data-element level: when multiple reports reference the same event, an IIS may combine information from administered and historical submissions (e.g., appending a missing site or route received later from a historical report to an administered record) according to local rules and provenance confidence. Implementers should treat the following as explanatory guidance rather than normative requirements: IIS commonly prefer the administering organization's record as the primary source when reconciling duplicate reports of the same vaccination event, and IIS MAY retain historical records alongside administered records when provenance cannot be confidently resolved.

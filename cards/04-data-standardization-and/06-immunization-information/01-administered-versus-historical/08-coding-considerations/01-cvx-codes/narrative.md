The CVX code set maintained by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention distinguishes between active and inactive codes. This distinction reflects whether a vaccine is currently authorized for use in the United States-not whether it is valid for historical reporting.

Administered vaccinations reported contemporaneously are generally expected to use CVX codes that are active at the time of administration. IIS do not expect foreign-administered doses to be reported as administered events in U.S. jurisdictions.

Historical vaccinations, by contrast, may legitimately use both active and inactive CVX codes.

Use of Unspecified Formulation CVX codes is acceptable for historical vaccinations when product detail is unknown.

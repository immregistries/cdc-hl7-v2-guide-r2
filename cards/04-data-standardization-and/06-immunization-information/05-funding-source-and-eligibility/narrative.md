Funding eligibility and funding source describe different aspects of how publicly supplied vaccine is used. Funding eligibility captures whether the patient and the specific vaccine dose qualified for a publicly funded program at the time of administration; it is a dose-level attribute tied to vaccine type and the time of administration. Funding source captures which inventory stock actually supplied the administered vaccine. Both concepts are needed to support Vaccines for Children (VFC) and other U.S. federal, state, and local publicly funded vaccine programs.

This information is primarily used for inventory decrementing and program operations, not for clinical decision support or national-level reporting. Implementations must preserve what actually occurred, even when it does not align with program expectations, so that program managers can detect and correct policy or workflow issues without distorting the patient's immunization record.

Submitting systems SHALL be capable of reporting both funding eligibility and funding source for administered vaccinations when such information is known (see HL7 field references). IIS SHALL be capable of receiving and storing both funding eligibility and funding source for administered vaccinations, including the specific vaccine type or product for each administered dose, since eligibility may vary by vaccine (for example, some travel vaccines are not covered).

IIS and submitting systems SHALL retain funding eligibility and funding source as part of the immunization event record to support program evaluation and inventory reconciliation. These elements are dose-level and encounter-specific; they SHOULD NOT be assumed to persist across future encounters and SHOULD NOT be relied upon as the sole basis for long-term clinical decision support.

**Note on terminology:** This guide uses HL7 terminology as the primary labels for requirements and normative statements. Equivalent or related terms are used in other sources: the CDC Core Data Elements for Functional Standards resource refers to "Dose-Level Program Eligibility" and "Funding Source/Status," and relevant LOINC codes include Vaccine Funding Program Eligibility (64994-7) and Vaccine Funding Source (30963-3).

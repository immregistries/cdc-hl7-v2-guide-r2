Nationally defined codes exist for common VFC-related eligibility categories. Jurisdictions that operate additional programs funded by federal, state, or local sources may need to define additional eligibility codes to support them. These local codes must be clearly distinguishable and must not duplicate concepts already defined in the national code set.

IIS SHALL use nationally defined funding eligibility and funding source codes where such codes exist for the jurisdiction's programs. This includes nationally defined federal-level codes used by multi-jurisdictional providers, such as V01, V02, and V03.

A federal provider, for instance, may use federal-level codes such as V01 (Not VFC eligible) for eligibility and VXC50 (Public) for funding source, since doses may be sourced from direct federal inventory rather than a jurisdictional inventory; jurisdictions and multi-jurisdictional providers should publish any local mapping or handling instructions relevant to scenarios like this one.

Submitting systems that support local eligibility codes SHALL send those codes using the assigning authority specified by the IIS.

Represents a vaccination that was considered but not administered due to a contraindication. This Order Segment Group links contraindication observations to a specific vaccine that was not given.

The following LOINC-coded observations may appear in OBX segments within this group:

Contraindication / precaution - LOINC **30945-0**

Contraindication effective date - LOINC **30946-8**

Contraindication expiration date - LOINC **30944-3**

Free-text comment - LOINC **48767-8**

ORC and RXA population rules for contraindicated event groups are defined in Section 4.8.1, Conditions (Contraindications, Precautions, and Indications) .

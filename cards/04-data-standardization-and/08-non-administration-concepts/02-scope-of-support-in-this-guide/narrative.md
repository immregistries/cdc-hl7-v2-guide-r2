This guide standardizes the reporting of patient-level observations and refusals. It also permits submission of dose-level non-administration messages but does not assign interoperable meaning to the non-administration itself.

Patient-level observations, such as conditions, serology results, and contraindications, do not inherit semantics from the ORC/RXA group in which they are transmitted, and must therefore be treated as independent clinical facts regardless of whether they appear inside an administered RXA or a non-administration RXA. The association between a condition and a non-administered vaccine is not standardized and must not be relied upon.

- IIS are NOT expected to store or operationalize the fact that a vaccine was not given.

Refusals must identify both when the vaccination was declined and what vaccination was refused. The minimum required elements are:

ORC-3=9999 – Filler Order Number.

RXA-3 - Date the vaccination would have been administered if accepted.

RXA-5 - Vaccine that was offered and refused.

RXA-18 - Reason for refusal

RXA-20 = "RE" - Indicator that the vaccination was refused

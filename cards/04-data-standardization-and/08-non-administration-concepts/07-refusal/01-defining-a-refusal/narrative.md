A **refusal** represents an intentional declination of a vaccination that was both due and available for administration at that encounter. It should not be used to document missed appointments, vaccine unavailability, shared decision-making deferrals, or clinical contraindications.

A VXU may contain zero administered vaccinations (vaccination cardinality [0..\*]) and may be used to submit refusal information only.

Contextual Conditions are not part of the canonical immunization history and are not considered part of the IIS core intent. IIS are not obligated to store them, act on them, or return errors when they are unsupported. IIS may choose to retain Contextual Conditions that are useful for clinical decision support, forecasting, or reporting, subject to local policy, legal, and regulatory constraints.

Contextual Conditions can be valuable because they may influence forecasting or clinical decision support, for example, serological evidence of immunity, documented contraindications, or chronic conditions that affect schedule recommendations. However, IIS cannot be relied upon to maintain a complete or current set of these conditions; clinical responsibility for identifying and evaluating contraindications and precautions remains with the clinician.

Most non-administration concepts standardized by this guide are represented as patient-level observations and reported using an ORC/RXA group that explicitly indicates that no vaccination event occurred.

Refusals are handled separately because they represent a consent-related encounter decision.

Although HL7 v2 allows conditions to be associated with non-administered doses, this guide does not define interoperable semantics for that association. Implementers must not infer that such linkage conveys standardized meaning or that IIS will preserve it.

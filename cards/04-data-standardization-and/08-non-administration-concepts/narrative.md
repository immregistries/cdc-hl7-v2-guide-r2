Non-administration in immunization exchange refers broadly to situations in which a vaccine dose is not administered to a patient. These situations arise for different reasons and do not represent a single, well-defined data concept. This guide therefore distinguishes among several non-administration-related concepts and clarifies which are standardized, which may be exchanged without defined meaning, and which fall outside the scope of interoperable IIS behavior.

This distinction is necessary because HL7 v2 allows expression of non-administration in multiple ways, but IIS programs have not established consistent requirements for collecting, storing, or acting on this information. As a result, the presence of non-administration data in a message must not be assumed to imply shared semantics or downstream use.

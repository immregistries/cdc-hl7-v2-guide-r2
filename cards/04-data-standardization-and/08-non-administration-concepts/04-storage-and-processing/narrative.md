None of the non-administration concepts described in this section are considered Critical Data. IIS are therefore not obligated to store them, act on them, or return errors when they are unsupported. This differs from administered or historical vaccinations, where IIS are expected to validate, store, and report acceptance or rejection.

IIS are not authoritative repositories for refusals or conditions and cannot be relied upon to maintain a complete record of these data.

Some non-administration concepts, particularly refusals, serology or evidence of immunity, and documented history of disease, are clinically significant and commonly used by CDS engines. While this guide does not mandate IIS storage for these items, many jurisdictions do store and operationalize them. Jurisdictions that expect storage or use of these concepts SHOULD document their expectations in their local implementation guide and onboarding materials.

EHR certification programs may also require submitting systems to send refusals and related data to public health. This guide's permissive storage stance does not alter those certification requirements; jurisdictions decide whether and how their IIS store such submissions.

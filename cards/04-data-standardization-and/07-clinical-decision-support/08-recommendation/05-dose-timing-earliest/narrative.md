Forecast dates communicate when a dose may be given, when it should ideally be given, and, in optional cases, when it becomes overdue or when administration is no longer recommended. Earliest and recommended dates express the clinically relevant window for administration. Overdue and latest dates may be provided but vary in definition across CDS implementations: the meaning of an overdue date often requires discussion with the registry, while a latest date may reflect a schedule's maximum-age constraint for certain vaccines and can be authoritative for those vaccines.

Recommendation information communicates forecast outcomes, for example, doses due, complete, overdue, too early, immune, age-inappropriate, too old, or not yet started, for each immunization series applicable to the patient; the listed outcomes are illustrative rather than exhaustive. Forecast information is generated at the time of the query and reflects the patient's age, dose history, and any relevant clinical factors supported by the registry or the registry's CDS engine. Forecast results must be returned in a structured, predictable form so receiving systems can process them consistently. This section defines how recommendation information is expressed; it does not dictate the clinical logic used to produce it.

Specific areas where further guidance may be developed include requiring reasons for recommendations in certain cases, and clarifying or adding additional status categories within immunization series. Current guidance recommends using LOINC Answer codes for status; future guidance may expand or refine these LOINC Answer codes or introduce additional interoperable codes to capture greater status granularity.

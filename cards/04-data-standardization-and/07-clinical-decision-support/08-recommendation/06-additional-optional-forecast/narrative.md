Additional fields may be included to describe how the CDS engine interpreted the patient's status. These fields are useful but not standardized, and their meaning should be treated as explanatory rather than authoritative. Series name, total number of doses in series, and dose number of next event may help clarify CDS behavior but may not align across implementations. Schedule used is similarly optional; only one schedule code is defined today (ACIP), and it does not capture local adaptations that are common in practice.

CDS output must address every standard routine (age-based) immunization recommendation used by the registry's CDS logic. For each standard recommendation, the RSP includes both historical evaluations and a corresponding recommendation, for example, even when the series is complete, the patient is too old, the patient is too young, or no further doses are required. This ensures receiving systems can consistently interpret whether a recommendation is complete, still active, overdue, too early to begin, or age-inappropriate. It also ensures consistency across patients and minimizes ambiguity in downstream decision-making.

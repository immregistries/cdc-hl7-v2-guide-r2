Clinical Decision Support (CDS) information in an RSP message communicates how a patient's immunization history was assessed and what future vaccinations are recommended. CDS has two complementary components: evaluation, which indicates how each vaccination counts toward one or more immunization series, and recommendation, which provides the forecast indicating recommended vaccinations based on patient age, vaccination history, and any relevant clinical factors the registry supports, such as documented contraindications, precautions, or immunity.

CDS results are generated at the time of the query and reflect the system's current interpretation of ACIP-based logic. The information returned is intentionally comprehensive, including items that may be clinically relevant now (e.g., an overdue vaccination), clinically relevant later (e.g., earliest and recommended dates), or informational only (e.g., a series marked complete for a vaccine no longer age-appropriate). Because CDS output is used in varied contexts, receiving systems may need to filter or suppress content that is not relevant to the encounter.

This guide defines how CDS information must be expressed in the RSP message; it does not prescribe the underlying logic. That logic is determined by the CDS system implemented by the registry and must be routinely updated in alignment with nationally or jurisdictionally recognized immunization recommendations.

As CDS support matures, this guide provides more consistent requirements for encoding evaluation and recommendation data. Future versions of the guide are expected to continue tightening requirements as community implementation patterns stabilize and as more registries adopt uniform CDS capabilities.

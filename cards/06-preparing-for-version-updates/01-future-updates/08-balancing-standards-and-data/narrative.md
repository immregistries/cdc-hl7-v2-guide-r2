A key responsibility of systems is to balance adherence to standards with maintaining and improving data quality. Overly rigid enforcement of specific HL7 rules, particularly those that do not directly impact the accuracy or utility of data, can lead to unnecessary loss of information. Instead, systems should prioritize functionality that ensures:

**Accurate and Complete Data:** Focusing on capturing and exchanging high-quality immunization data.

**Interoperability:** Supporting exchange with partners operating on older versions or with minor variances in implementation.

**Program Support:** Ensuring that data supports public health programs and goals, rather than creating barriers to collaboration.

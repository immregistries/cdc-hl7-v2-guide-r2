IIS and their partners should take a thoughtful, coordinated approach to deploying changes required by the updated guide.

- **Minimize Disruption:** Make only the minimum changes necessary to meet the requirements of the updated standards.
- **Communicate Early:** Share planned changes with partners as early as possible to allow sufficient time for them to prepare and adapt. Even minor changes can have significant downstream impacts.
- **Collaborate with Partners:** Engage in open communication to ensure that system upgrades are aligned with the needs and capabilities of exchange partners.
- **Provide Test Environments:** Offer robust testing environments where partners can validate changes before production deployment. Ensure that system vendors (such as EHR vendors) also have access to these test environments.
- **Develop Transition Plans:** Create clear migration plans to help partners phase in updates over time.
- **Offer Training and Resources:** Provide resources, documentation, and training to help partners understand and implement changes effectively.

By following these principles, the immunization community can ensure that updates to the guide enhance interoperability, improve data quality, and support public health goals while minimizing disruption to existing operations.

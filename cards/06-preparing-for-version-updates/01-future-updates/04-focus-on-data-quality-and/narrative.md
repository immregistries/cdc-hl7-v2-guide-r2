As systems plan their upgrades, they should prioritize changes that support data quality and program requirements. Conformance to HL7 standards is a means to an end and should be approached with the goal of improving data accuracy, completeness, and usability. Systems should avoid enforcing arbitrary HL7 rules that do not contribute to these objectives. Instead, focus should be placed on rules and requirements that directly enhance interoperability and support programmatic goals.

For example:

**Data Quality:** Systems should prioritize functionality that enhances the quality of exchanged data over strict enforcement of minor formatting rules.

**Policy and Program Support:** Implementers should align system functionality with broader public health goals and regulatory requirements.

Implementers should prioritize upgrading their systems to align with this updated version of the HL7 v2.5.1 Implementation Guide for Immunization Messaging, Release 2.0 , based on the HL7 v2.5.1 base standard, as soon as possible. This version is designed to reduce costs and ambiguity in data transmission, improve data quality, enable advanced operations, and support current efforts to collect and maintain high-quality immunization data. Over time, compliance with this version will become a key expectation for systems, and early adoption will provide significant operational benefits.

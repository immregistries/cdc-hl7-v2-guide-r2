TABs are scoped specifically to HL7 v2.5.1 immunization data exchange and:

**Focus on Technical Specifications:** Provide detailed guidance for interoperability at the technical level.

**Policy and Regulation:** Support, but do not specify, policy or regulatory requirements. TABs provide the technical foundation necessary for systems to meet policy and regulatory goals. However, they do not define, add, or enforce policy or regulation beyond ensuring compliance with standards when they are implemented.

**Support Future Development:** Guide the inclusion of new information in subsequent updates to the National Implementation Guide.

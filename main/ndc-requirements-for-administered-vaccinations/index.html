Vaccines with Multiple NDCs

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL support Unit-of-Use NDCs for administered vaccinations.s1
  2. SHOULDSubmitting systems SHOULD support Unit-of-Sale NDCs for administered vaccinations.s2
  3. SHALLIIS SHALL accept both Unit-of-Use and Unit-of-Sale NDCs when reported.s3
  4. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL support NDC11 formatted with dashes using the 5-4-2 format (xxxxx-xxxx-xx).s4
  5. SHOULD NOTSubmitting systems SHOULD NOT use NDC10 without dashes.s5
  6. MAYSubmitting systems MAY support NDC11 without dashes.s6
  7. MAYSubmitting systems MAY support NDC10 with dashes using the 4-4-2 format.s7
  8. SHALLIIS SHALL be capable of accepting and interpreting commonly used NDC formats.s8
  9. SHALLFor vaccines requiring reconstitution, submitting systems SHALL report the NDC for the lyophilized powder (the active product) rather than the diluent.s9
  10. SHALLFor vaccines with multiple active components, submitting systems SHALL report any NDC associated with the vaccine (Unit-of-Use or Unit-of-Sale), consistent with clinical workflow.s10
  11. SHALLIIS SHALL accept administered vaccination reports for multi-component products without requiring a specific NDC selection.s11

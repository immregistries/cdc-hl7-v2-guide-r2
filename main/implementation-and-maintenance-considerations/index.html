Organizational structures evolve over time as clinics merge, rename, split, or change ownership. These operational changes must be reflected in IIS organization identifiers to maintain correct attribution and program accountability. Maintaining accurate organization identifiers requires ongoing coordination between IIS and submitting organizations. These identifiers are managed per connection or onboarding arrangement and are not required to be publicly published.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS and submitters SHALL maintain a shared record of organization identifiers used in immunization messages.s1
  2. SHOULDIIS SHOULD provide documented processes for requesting, updating, or retiring identifiers.s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY simplify complex local structures to maintain stable and manageable identifiers.s3
  4. SHOULDSubmitters SHOULD notify IIS of organizational changes that affect ownership or structure.s4

4.6.1.7 EHR Workflow Considerations

Submission systems typically distinguish administered and historical vaccinations to separate billable clinical activity from medical history documentation. These workflows differ substantially and influence data completeness and coding choices.

Administered vaccinations are typically entered through structured clinical workflows tied to billing, inventory, and clinical decision support.

Historical vaccinations are typically entered through streamlined history-capture workflows designed for rapid entry with minimal required detail.

2.7.7 Use in Query Transactions (QBP/RSP)

Organizations are also identified when querying the IIS. In query transactions, MSH-4 and MSH-22 serve the same purposes as in VXU messages: identifying the querying organization and establishing authorization to access patient data. Query transactions operate at the organization level, using MSH-4 (Submitting Organization) and MSH-22 (Responsible Organization) to identify the entity authorized to perform the query. These identifiers represent the organization responsible for the connection and authorization to access IIS data, and are not intended to identify individual facilities or child organizational units. This is noted as a current limitation of the specification.

Organization identifiers included in the original message, such as MSH-4 and MSH-22, are typically mirrored back to the sender as part of the message envelope in acknowledgement responses. This preserves organization identification across the request, response, and acknowledgement exchange, allowing implementers to track which organizations were involved throughout the transaction.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLQuery messages SHALL include MSH-4 to identify the querying organization.⚑s1
  2. SHALLMSH-22 SHALL be included when the querying organization is acting on behalf of another responsible organization.⚑s2
  3. SHOULDWhen returning administered vaccination records in an RSP message, IIS SHOULD include the administering organization identifier RXA-11.4 and, when available, the entering organization identifier ORC-17, provided the IIS has clear, coded values that can be meaningfully interpreted by the requesting system.s3

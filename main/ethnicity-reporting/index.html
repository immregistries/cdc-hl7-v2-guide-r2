  • Note 3 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3.qf-statement-from-paragraph-12a6070f

Ethnicity is a mutually exclusive category under current standards. Unlike race, it is not multi-valued in HL7 v2 immunization messaging.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLEthnicity SHALL be reported in PID-22.⚑s1
  2. SHALL NOTMultiple repetitions of PID-22 SHALL NOT be used.⚑s2
  3. MAYIf ethnicity is unknown or not provided, PID-22 MAY be omitted or populated with UNK, ASKU, or PHC1175.⚑s3

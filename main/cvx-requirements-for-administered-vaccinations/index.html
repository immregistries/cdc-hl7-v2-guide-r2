4.6.2.4.2 CVX Requirements for Administered Vaccinations

Quality review 1 warning

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning Statement s3 Statement combines SHALL, SHALL NOT. Each statement should carry one requirement so it can be tested and referenced on its own.qf-multi-verb-statement-4c07057e

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL support Active U.S. vaccine CVX codes for administered vaccinations.s1
  2. SHALL NOTSubmitting systems SHALL NOT use Never Active CVX codes to report administered vaccinations.s2
  3. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL support the non-vaccine CVX code 998 only for HL7 structural purposes and SHALL NOT use it to represent an administered vaccination.⚑s3
  4. SHALLIIS SHALL accept administered vaccination records reported with valid Active CVX codes.s4

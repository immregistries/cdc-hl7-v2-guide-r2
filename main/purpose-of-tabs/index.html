6.2.1 Purpose of TABs

The primary purposes of TABs are to:

Clarify Standards: Address interpretation and implementation questions related to the HL7 v2.5.1 standard.

Enhance Requirements: Apply updated HL7 v2 conformance methodology concepts to tighten and improve the documentation of requirements.

Provide Specifications: Offer detailed technical guidance for data exchange, ensuring compliance with the HL7 v2.5.1 standard.

Incorporate Community Input: TABs are developed with input from the IIS community and approved by the Standards and Interoperability Steering Committee (SISC), ensuring they address practical needs.

Inform Future Guides: Serve as a basis for future updates to the National Implementation Guide, ensuring continuous improvement of the standard.

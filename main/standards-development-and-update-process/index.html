6.3.1 Standards Development and Update Process

To ensure the guide remains current and aligned with the needs of the IIS and EHR communities, updates follow a structured process.

Drafting. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the American Immunization Registry Association (AIRA) draft updates in collaboration with the IIS community. Community involvement is prioritized to ensure guidance reflects real-world needs. Interim updates are often published in the form of Technical Advisory Bulletins (TABs), providing actionable guidance on specific topics.

Community Review. Draft documents are reviewed during Standards and Interoperability Steering Committee (SISC) meetings, where feedback is incorporated. The IIS community has an opportunity to comment and influence the final guidance.

Selective Implementation. TABs may recommend immediate adoption of certain functionalities, such as support for new coded values, that are not yet formally balloted. TABs represent a consensus direction and prepare the community for future guide updates.

HL7 Balloting. Once sufficient updates have been aggregated, a new version of the guide is formally balloted through HL7 (HL7's formal community voting process for approving changes to a standard before they become part of an official published version). This ensures broader review and approval by the health IT community.

Federal Standards Advancement and Regulation. Updates to HL7 standards undergo the Standards Version Advancement Process (SVAP) (a federal process through which updated versions of a standard are formally recognized for potential use in meeting regulatory requirements), positioning them for potential inclusion in federal regulations. However, inclusion in SVAP does not guarantee regulatory adoption, providing flexibility for the community to adapt as needed.

Future versions of this guide may be considered through applicable federal standards-advancement processes where appropriate. Any decisions about applicability or timing are determined independently of this implementation guide.

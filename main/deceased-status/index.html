4.5.7.3 Deceased Status

Quality review 1 warning · 4 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning Statement s4 Statement combines MAY, SHOULD NOT. Each statement should carry one requirement so it can be tested and referenced on its own.qf-multi-verb-statement-4a8ab010
  • Note Statement s1 Its subject is "Deceased status", not a system or party, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-2ce664c2
  • Note Statement s4 Its subject is "Receipt of a new immunization event", not a system or party, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-bca18f83
  • Note Statement s5 Its subject is "Such cases", not a system or party, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-eebb8c51
  • Note 5 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3, s4, s5.qf-statement-from-paragraph-c47303c1

Deceased status has clearer operational significance than active or inactive designations. It supports suppression of reminder/recall activities and helps IIS maintain accurate denominators. Deceased information may be reported by providers or derived from external death reporting sources.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLDeceased status SHALL take precedence over active or inactive designations.⚑s1
  2. SHOULDIIS SHOULD preserve deceased status consistently across provider-level and jurisdictional views of the patient.s2
  3. SHALLIIS SHALL continue to accept historical immunization data for deceased patients when submitted.s3
  4. MAYReceipt of a new immunization event MAY be interpreted by IIS as evidence of current engagement, but SHOULD NOT override an explicitly reported deceased status.⚑s4
  5. SHOULDSuch cases SHOULD be flagged for review to resolve possible matching or data-quality issues, and to determine whether the event represents historical data reported after the date of death.⚑s5

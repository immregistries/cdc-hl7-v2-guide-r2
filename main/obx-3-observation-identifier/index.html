OBX-3 identifies the observation being conveyed and SHALL contain a LOINC code. In immunization messaging, LOINC is used exclusively to define the meaning of OBX observations. The LOINC code represents the "question" being asked, while the remaining OBX fields carry the answer and its context.

This guide documents a defined subset of LOINC codes that IIS are expected to recognize and process. While LOINC supports a broad and extensible vocabulary, OBX-3 values used for immunization messaging SHALL be drawn from LOINC to ensure consistent interpretation across systems.

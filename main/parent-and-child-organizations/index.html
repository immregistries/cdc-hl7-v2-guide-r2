2.7.6 Parent and Child Organizations

Organizations are often structured hierarchically. A child organization is a designated unit of a parent organization, such as a clinic within a health system or a store within a pharmacy chain. These parent-child relationships are common in operational practice but may be represented differently by submitters and IIS.

The IIS is the authoritative source for organization identifiers and determines how parent and child organizations are represented for program operations. During onboarding, IIS staff and submitters jointly establish which organizational units require distinct identifiers and how those identifiers relate to one another, since the level of organizational granularity required for reporting may vary across jurisdictions and program operations. When a reporting workflow requires identification of specific organizational units within a larger organization, those identifiers are conveyed through the established organization fields in the message, specifically RXA-11.4 for the administering organization and ORC-17 for the organization that entered the record. These fields provide the appropriate mechanism for representing child organizational units when that level of detail is operationally necessary.

Whether child organizations must be represented in the message at all is determined jointly by the IIS and the submitting organization during onboarding; this section does not require that level of detail in every case. When child organizational units do need to be distinguished, however, the HL7 v2 message uses these established organization identifier fields rather than an alternative mechanism.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL assign and manage unique organization identifiers for onboarded entities.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL recognize that a single submitting or responsible organization may encompass multiple child organizations.s2
  3. MAYChild organizations MAY be distinguished using ORC-17 or RXA-11 when operationally necessary.s3
  4. SHOULDIIS SHOULD capture only the level of hierarchy needed for accountability and program operations.s4

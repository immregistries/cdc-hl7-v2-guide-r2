While systems are required to adhere to the updated standard, they are also expected to support the previous version of the guide. This ensures continuity and allows for a smoother transition for partners who may need time to upgrade. Implementers should recognize that minor issues in data format or adherence to standards should not automatically result in rejection of data if the data is otherwise usable and supports public health goals.

What was asked

Questions about the insurance segments come from jurisdictions considering collecting insurance data (often linked to vaccine funding changes) and ask whether other IIS support IN1-IN3, what code sets identify insurers and plans, and how local IGs should document an unsupported optional group. The consistent finding is that very few IIS support the insurance group, EHRs are not required to send it, and the IG gives little guidance beyond IN1.

IN1 / Insurance Group (general)

Do other IIS accept the insurance segments (IN1, IN2, IN3) in VXU, how do they use the data, and what guidance or lessons learned exist for a jurisdiction planning to add them (e.g., when moving to universal vaccine funding or to reduce manual entry errors)?

Asked 3× · 2022–2026 · VXU · Answered · Related: IN2, IN3; Local Profiles & Jurisdictional Constraints; Code Sets & Vocabulary

Answer given

AIRA is not aware of IIS that currently support the insurance segments in HL7. A July 2022 Information Request on this topic received 3 responses, all indicating they do not support the insurance segments. A few IIS collected insurance information for similar purposes around 2010-2014 (discussed in SISC), but little has been heard since.

The insurance group (IN1, IN2, IN3) is optional in the national IG; if the insurance group is included, IN1 is required. The IG gives no guidance on IN2 or IN3. EHRs are not required to include the group in VXUs under the certified health IT program, so providers would likely need their vendors to add support, which may make collecting it challenging.

The IN1 definition is on printed page 72 (digital page 80) of the HL7 2.5.1 Release 1.5 (2018 update) IG. It uses user-defined tables (e.g., 0072, 0086) that should be reviewed, and the Insurance Company ID may require local negotiation to identify insurance companies unless a known national code system exists in the payer space.

The 2022 question also asked whether there is a standard for insurance company ID/name and plan ID/name; no direct answer was recorded then, and the 2026 answer above (local negotiation unless a national payer code system exists) is the closest guidance.

A local IG changes IN1 segment usage from R to IX. Is this a conformance concern?

Asked 1× · 2024 · VXU · Answered · Related: IN2, IN3; Local Profiles & Jurisdictional Constraints

Answer given

IN1 belongs to the Insurance group, which is Optional (O) in the national IG. IN1 is required only when insurance information is collected. The local IG probably marked the Insurance group and the IN1, IN2, and IN3 segments as IX because the IIS does not collect that optional group. This is about as good as possible when an optional group is not supported, and it sets clear expectations.

TA Questions

Working analysis for the authors, from questions asked of AIRA's technical assistance service. Not part of the guide.

Implementers have asked AIRA's technical assistance service about HL7 v2 immunization messaging for ten years. The guide should be able to answer those questions, directly or by appeal to its rules and principles. These pages go through each question and record whether the current guide answers it, next to the answer given at the time. The past answers are not reviewed here: they are there to copy into the guide, or to show where the guide now says something different. Who asked is left out: it does not matter to the guide.

916 questions, consolidated from the TA records, grouped by segment and field and then by topic. 39 assessed so far. Left out: 9 questions about the Simple Message Mover (SMM), which is not in this guide's scope, and 98 one-off troubleshooting questions that do not generalize. Questions the guide does not fully answer also appear as quality findings (category "ta") on the section where the answer belongs.

What the results mean

  • Gap The guide does not address the question.
  • Partly answers The guide answers part of the question.
  • Differs The guide answers it differently from a past answer: a deliberate change, or a past answer to correct.
  • By appeal The answer follows from the guide's rules or principles, but the guide does not say it.
  • Answers The guide answers the question directly.
  • Out of scope Local policy, transport, testing tools, or one product: not for this guide.

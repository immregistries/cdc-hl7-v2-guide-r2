A report that a specific vaccine dose was not administered at a particular encounter.

Examples include:

A vaccine not given due to a condition

A vaccine offered and declined (refusal)

Dose-level non-administration is encounter-specific and vaccine-specific. While HL7 v2 permits these events to be messaged, IIS have not established consistent expectations for storing or interpreting them.

4.5.7.2 Active and Inactive Status

Quality review 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note 2 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2.qf-statement-from-paragraph-d4f91453

Active and inactive status values were originally designed to support a "medical home" model, in which a provider explicitly managed a defined patient panel and coordinated preventive services. While this model remains conceptually valid, modern EHR-centric workflows rarely include explicit status verification or reporting as part of routine immunization exchange.

Inactive status may be reported for a variety of reasons (e.g., patient moved, lost to follow-up), but these distinctions are often operationally indistinguishable to IIS and should not be over-interpreted.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT rely on active or inactive status as a prerequisite for accepting immunization events.s1
  2. SHOULDIIS SHOULD treat active and inactive values as advisory rather than authoritative.s2

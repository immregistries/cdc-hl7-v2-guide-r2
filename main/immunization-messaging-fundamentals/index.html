2 Immunization Messaging Fundamentals

Reliable data exchange depends on a shared foundation for how Immunization Information Systems (IIS) and their partners structure, transmit, and confirm messages. This section defines that foundation. It describes the standards that govern message content, the transport mechanisms that carry them, and the acknowledgement processes that confirm their receipt and quality. Together, these fundamentals establish a consistent national framework that reduces interface variation, improves data reliability, and supports timely, accurate immunization reporting across all participating systems.

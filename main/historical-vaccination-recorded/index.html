3.2.5.2 Historical Vaccination Recorded

Historical vaccinations are doses administered outside the submitting system and later transcribed into the patient record based on available documentation or clinical determination. A vaccination may be recorded as historical when the clinician has sufficient information to reasonably conclude that the vaccination occurred, including the type of vaccine administered and the date of administration.

Historical vaccinations SHOULD NOT be submitted/reported to the IIS when the vaccine type or administration date cannot be determined. The standard supports the reporting of non-specific vaccine information when needed, including the use of appropriate unspecified formulation vaccine codes, and accepts vaccinations administered long ago or outside the United States. In some cases, such as combination vaccines not used domestically, it may be appropriate to record a single historical vaccination as multiple component vaccinations to accurately represent the immunization history.

Any vaccination administered to a living patient at any point in their lifetime, regardless of location, is eligible to be reported as a historical vaccination when adequate information is available. Recording a complete and accurate immunization history is essential to enable correct evaluation, forecasting, and clinical decision support within the IIS.

Conformance statements

  1. SHOULDThe submitting system SHOULD trigger a VXU when a historical vaccination is recorded.s1
  2. SHALLThe vaccination event SHALL be clearly identified as historical using the vaccination event record type (RXA-9) indicator.⚑s2

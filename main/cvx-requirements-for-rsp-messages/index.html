4.6.2.6.1 CVX Requirements for RSP Messages

Quality review 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note 4 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3, s4.qf-statement-from-paragraph-c2036099

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL return a CVX code for every vaccination included in an RSP immunization history.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL be capable of returning Active, Inactive, and Non-US CVX codes in RSP messages.s2
  3. SHOULDQuerying systems SHOULD expect to receive Active, Inactive, and Non-US CVX codes in immunization history responses.s3
  4. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT return Never Active CVX codes in RSP messages.s4

Jurisdictions have the responsibility for ensuring that the recommendations they return are appropriate for their population. This guide does not specify the schedule requirements. Instead, it specifies which series require a recommendation entry and how those recommendations must be expressed in the RSP.

The structural requirements of this guide apply uniformly to age-based recommendations, that is, recommendations whose eligibility and timing are determined primarily by patient age rather than by individual risk factors or clinical conditions:

This approach ensures interoperability and clarity while preserving jurisdictional autonomy in determining the clinical content of recommendations.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLWhen an RSP message includes evaluations and recommendations they SHALL cover all age-based recommendations used by the registry’s CDS logic.⚑s1

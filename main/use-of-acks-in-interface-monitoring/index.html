3.3.5 Use of ACKs in Interface Monitoring

ACK messages provide structured signals about both individual transactions and aggregate interface performance [6]. While this section defines interpretation of a single ACK, consistent generation and interpretation across systems enables scalable monitoring of data quality and operational stability.

ACKs are not intended to enforce perfection or report every possible conformance deviation. Their purpose is to highlight conditions that materially affect the IIS's ability to maintain a complete and accurate immunization history.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL ensure that severities assigned in ACK messages accurately reflect expected follow-up actions.s1
  2. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT use rejection solely as a mechanism to enforce minor conformance preferences that do not result in loss of Critical Data or failure to fulfill core intent.s2
  3. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL analyze ACK feedback in aggregate to identify recurring data quality patterns.s3

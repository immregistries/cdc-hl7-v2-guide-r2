2.8.1 Message Header Date/Time (MSH-7)

MSH-7 identifies when the sending system created the message. This timestamp supports operational processing, auditing, and troubleshooting, but it is not used for clinical interpretation of immunization events. Because systems operate across multiple platforms and time zones, consistent timestamp precision and offset handling are required to ensure interoperability.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYSubmitting systems MAY use any valid time zone offset and are not required to convert timestamps to the IIS local time zone.s1
  2. SHALLReceiving systems SHALL accept functionally equivalent timestamps expressed with different time zone offsets.s2
  3. SHALL NOTMSH-7 SHALL NOT be used for clinical interpretation, immunization evaluation, or forecasting.⚑s3

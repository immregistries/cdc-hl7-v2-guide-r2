QBP profiles define a standardized interface for retrieving immunization information from an IIS. Query operations are read-only by design: submitting a query does not create, modify, or delete patient or immunization data. Their purpose is to make existing IIS information available in a reliable and predictable manner.

These profiles support two distinct access patterns that serve different operational needs.

  • User-initiated queries are driven by a person actively requesting information for a specific patient and awaiting an immediate response. This is the primary use case for which IIS were created. Query volume in this model is naturally bounded by human interaction, and uninterrupted availability is critical; delays or degraded performance directly impact clinical workflows and decision-making at the point of care.
  • System-initiated queries are performed without a user waiting for an immediate result. These queries may operate on individual patients or on sets of patients and are typically used for planning, evaluation, or population-level activities. Because their scale and frequency are not inherently bounded, these queries must be designed and scheduled to avoid placing undue load on the IIS; they generally do not support interactive resolution of ambiguous matches and may tolerate delayed completion without clinical impact.

QBP profiles support retrieval of two categories of information:

  • Complete immunization history, representing all immunizations known to the IIS.
  • Evaluation and recommendations (forecast), generated by IIS clinical decision support using the schedule selected and maintained by the IIS and accurate as of the time of query.

Evaluation and forecast results are derived solely from the immunization history available to the IIS. They do not account for immunizations that are missing from the IIS record, nor do they consistently reflect patient-specific risk conditions that may alter recommended schedules. For this reason, evaluation and recommendation results are intended to support, rather than replace, clinical judgment. Clinicians should use IIS recommendations as a starting point, in conjunction with patient consultation and clinical knowledge, when determining which vaccinations to administer.

Query exchange for IIS-to-IIS and IZ Gateway transactions is out of scope for this guide. This guide may serve as a baseline, but jurisdictions must document additional expectations in local implementation guides and utilize IZ Gateway Companion Guides. See Section 1.1, Key Objectives, for the full scope statement.

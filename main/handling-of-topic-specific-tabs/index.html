While most TABs provide general guidance applicable to all systems implementing the current guide, some focus on specific topics. (for example, Guidance on Messaging Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity) These topic-specific TABs include SHALL requirements for when implementation is required by law or policy. If an organization decides to exchange data specified by these TABs, they SHALL follow the guidance precisely as defined. TABs specify the technical methods for data exchange but do not establish or prohibit the collection or sharing of data, which remains a separate policy decision.

4.7.7.4 Dose Validity and Reason for Validity

Quality review 1 issue · 1 warning · 6 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors. Not part of the guide.

  • Issue Statement s3 TAB CDS in RSP Alignment (Table 2: 59781-5 Dose validity): Conflict. The guide says "If dose validity cannot be determined or is unavailable at query time, the dose-validity OBX ... SHALL be omitted" (stated twice, in 4.7.7.4 s3 and in 4.7.7.1 "Future work" s2). The TAB requires dose validity for every evaluation, and so does IGAMT (59781-5 is R in the Z42 evaluation group). The Y/N rule matches; the order after the vaccine type is in narrative only.
    ConflictAction: CDC decision
    TAB Dose validity SHALL be returned, SHOULD come right after 30956-7 and before the other evaluation OBX segments, and SHALL be 'Y' if valid and 'N' if not. Only doses that are valid and count toward the final series are 'Y'; extraneous doses (CDSi) are 'N'.
    Recommendation Remove the omission rule (both copies), or take it to SISC as a change to the TAB and to IGAMT. Move the two statements in "Future work" (4.7.7.1) to the sections they belong to; that card is otherwise about future guidance.

    See the TAB comparison

    qf-tab-cds-08
  • Warning Statement s2 "Y or N are the only allowable values" for dose validity is not enforced. The dose-validity observation (59781-5) is typed ID with no value set in the Z32 and Z42 co-constraints, so any value passes.
    GapAction: IGAMT
    Narrative "'Y' or 'N' SHALL be used in OBX-5 as the only allowable values when dose validity is reported"
    IGAMT export
    • Z42 and Z32 Immunization Evaluation Results: 59781-5 OBX-2 = ID; no OBX-5 binding.
    • HL70136_IZ01 (Y, N) exists and is used for PD1-12.
    Recommendation Bind OBX-5 for 59781-5 to HL70136_IZ01 (or an equivalent Y/N value set).
    qf-igamt-dose-validity
  • Note MIROW guide MIROW Common Vocabulary (Dose Condition): Partly covered. The guide has no way for a submitter to say that a dose it gave was substandard (expired, stored out of range, or given incorrectly). An IIS that learns of it shows it only as an invalid dose in the evaluation of the RSP. MIROW's decrementing guide records such a dose as "administered, non-viable" (S501), and the deduplication guide keeps the repeat dose and flags the invalid one (mirow-vd-15). The usual practice is to report the dose as given and let the IIS or the forecaster judge it, which may be enough.
    Partly coveredAction: CDC decision
    Best practice An indicator that a dose administered to a patient is considered substandard.
    Recommendation CDC to decide whether the guide should say how a submitter reports a dose it knows to be substandard (for example, a dose later found to have been stored out of range), or state that this is left to the IIS's evaluation.

Dose validity communicates whether the vaccination counts toward completion of the evaluated series. When a dose is invalid, one or more reasons may be provided to clarify why.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLDose validity SHALL be reported using the designated LOINC observation identifier specified in Chapter 7 (OBX table). See Chapter 7 for the exact LOINC code(s) and value-set binding.⚑s1
  2. SHALL"Y" or "N" SHALL be used in OBX-5 as the only allowable values when dose validity is reported (Y = counts toward the evaluated series; N = does not count toward the evaluated series).⚑s2
  3. SHALLIf dose validity cannot be determined or is unavailable at query time, the dose-validity OBX for that evaluation instance SHALL be omitted.⚑s3
  4. SHOULDReason-for-validity SHOULD be reported when validity is "N."⚑s4
  5. SHALLWhen multiple reasons apply, additional OBX segments SHALL be used to repeat the reason.⚑s5

Dose validity communicates whether the vaccination counts toward completion of the evaluated series. When a dose is invalid, one or more reasons may be provided to clarify why.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLDose validity SHALL be reported using the designated LOINC observation identifier specified in Chapter 7 (OBX table). See Chapter 7 for the exact LOINC code(s) and value-set binding.⚑s1
  2. SHALL"Y" or "N" SHALL be used in OBX-5 as the only allowable values when dose validity is reported (Y = counts toward the evaluated series; N = does not count toward the evaluated series).⚑s2
  3. SHALLIf dose validity cannot be determined or is unavailable at query time, the dose-validity OBX for that evaluation instance SHALL be omitted.⚑s3
  4. SHOULDReason-for-validity SHOULD be reported when validity is "N."⚑s4
  5. SHALLWhen multiple reasons apply, additional OBX segments SHALL be used to repeat the reason.⚑s5

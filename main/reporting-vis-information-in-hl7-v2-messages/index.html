4.6.6.3 Reporting VIS Information in HL7 v2 Messages

  • Warning Z22 administered doses cannot carry the VIS publication date (29768-9) for the alternate VIS method. The alternate VIS method is a CVX (30956-7), the publication date (29768-9), and the date given (29769-7). In the Z22 administered-dose table, the "VIS Information: Vaccine Type and Dates" group lists 29769-7 twice and 29768-9 not at all. The historical-dose table has it right. The Z22 administered document-type group also has no OBX-5 binding for 69764-9; the historical group binds PHVS_VISBarcodes_IIS. Constraint IZ-36 covers the barcode check separately.

VIS information is reported using one or more OBX segments within the administered vaccination group (ORC/RXA/RXR). IIS may receive two different reporting methods and must support both.

Under the preferred method, submitters report the VIS barcode identifier as an OBX containing the VIS document identifier, followed by a separate OBX giving the date the VIS was provided to the patient. Under the alternate method, submitters report the CVX Unspecified Formulation code representing the applicable VIS, along with an OBX giving the VIS publication date and an OBX giving the VIS date-given. Both methods may appear in the same message, and IIS must be able to accept either approach.

A single administered vaccine may legitimately have multiple associated VIS statements, whether as one combination VIS or as multiple component VISes.

This is especially important for combination vaccines when only individual-component VIS documents are available, so that multiple VIS instances can be unambiguously associated with the single administered CVX product.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL support both VIS reporting methods.s1
  2. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL support at least one VIS reporting method.s2
  3. SHALLIIS SHALL accept VIS barcode identifiers even when the identifier is not recognized.s3
  4. SHALLIIS SHALL accept publication dates and dates-given even when they differ from expected values.s4
  5. SHALL NOTThe VIS date-given SHALL NOT be later than the vaccine administration date.⚑s5
  6. SHOULDIIS SHOULD follow up on unexpected VIS data through data-quality processes when programmatically needed.s6
  7. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT reject an administered vaccination due to problems with VIS identifiers, publication dates, or dates-given.s7
  8. SHALLSubmitting systems and IIS SHALL support multiple VIS entries per administered vaccine.s8
  9. SHOULDSubmitting systems SHOULD populate OBX-4 to group related VIS OBX segments for each VIS instance, and IIS SHOULD use OBX-4 when present to associate the VIS identifier, publication date, and date-given fields.⚑s9

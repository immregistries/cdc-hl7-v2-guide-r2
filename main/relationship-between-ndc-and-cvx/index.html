4.6.2.3.3 Relationship Between NDC and CVX

NDC and CVX serve complementary roles. NDC identifies the specific manufactured product used for an administered vaccination, while CVX represents the clinical concept of the vaccination received. Because NDC values change as products cycle through the market, they are not suitable for long-term immunization history without association to a CVX code.

IIS are therefore expected to use CVX as the canonical representation for immunization history while incorporating NDC when available to support traceability, reconciliation, and audit needs.

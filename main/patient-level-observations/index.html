4.8.1.1 Patient-Level Observations

Clinical facts about the patient that exist independently of a specific vaccination encounter and may influence immunization decisions across time or across multiple vaccines.

Examples include:

Conditions affecting vaccination decisions (e.g., pregnancy, allergy)

Documented immunity

Historical adverse events

These observations describe patient context. They do not assert that a vaccine was offered, withheld, or refused, and they do not represent immunization history.

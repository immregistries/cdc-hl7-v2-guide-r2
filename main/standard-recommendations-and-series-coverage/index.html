CDS output must address every standard routine (age-based) immunization recommendation used by the registry's CDS logic. For each standard recommendation, the RSP includes both historical evaluations and a corresponding recommendation, for example, even when the series is complete, the patient is too old, the patient is too young, or no further doses are required. This ensures receiving systems can consistently interpret whether a recommendation is complete, still active, overdue, too early to begin, or age-inappropriate. It also ensures consistency across patients and minimizes ambiguity in downstream decision-making.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLFor every applicable standard routine (age-based) recommendation, the RSP SHALL include evaluation information for all administered doses that may count toward that recommendation.⚑s1
  2. SHALLThe RSP SHALL also include a recommendation entry in the forecast for every such recommendation, even if the recommendation is complete, age-inappropriate, or no further doses are required (for example, because the patient is too old or too young).⚑s2
  3. SHALLForecast entries SHALL cover all standard routine recommendations; limiting the forecast to only those recommendations requiring another dose is not permitted.⚑s3

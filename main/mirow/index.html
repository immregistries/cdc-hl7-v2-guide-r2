MIROW Documents

AIRA's Modeling of Immunization Registry Operations Workgroup (MIROW) published best practice guides for IIS operations from 2005 to 2022. They do not define messages, but deduplication, record consolidation, patient status, inventory decrementing, and data quality all depend on what arrives in VXU and QBP messages and what is returned in ACK and RSP messages. These pages go through each guide's principles and business rules and record where they connect with this guide: whether the guide carries the data the practice needs, says the same thing, uses the same terms, and points to the MIROW guide where it should.

A practice that does not depend on what is exchanged (staffing, internal workflow, reporting) is not itemized. "Covered" means the guide supports the practice; "Missing" means the practice needs something from the message, or a statement or pointer in the guide, that the guide does not yet give.

Each document links to its published copy on the AIRA repository and to the copy kept with this guide's source. 10 of 10 documents have been compared so far. Results that need attention also appear as quality findings on the guide sections they concern.

What the results mean

  • Conflict The guide or the profiles say something different.
  • Missing Not in the guide.
  • Partly covered Some of it is in the guide.
  • Reworded The guide says it differently; check that the meaning is unchanged.
  • In narrative only The guide says it, but in narrative rather than as a numbered conformance statement.
  • Covered The guide says the same thing.
  • Guide adds A requirement in the guide that the document does not have.
  • Out of scope Outside the scope of this guide.

Health Information Exchanges (HIEs) may be involved in routing messages between submitting/querying systems and IIS. This guide does not prescribe whether data should go through an HIE or directly to an IIS. However, any intermediary must be transparent to the submitter. HL7 messages routed through an HIE must result in a final response generated by the IIS, conforming fully to the requirements of this guide and reflecting the true disposition of the message.

4.6.1.1 Administered Vaccinations

An administered vaccination is one given directly by the reporting organization. These records are first-hand clinical events and are typically captured as part of formal care workflows.

Administered records are expected to contain more complete and specific data and may drive additional IIS processing such as inventory decrementing or enhanced validation.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLAdministered vaccinations SHALL represent doses given directly by the reporting organization.⚑s1
  2. SHOULDAdministered records SHOULD include more complete product information when available (e.g., specific CVX, manufacturer, lot number, NDC).⚑s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY apply additional data quality or inventory rules to administered vaccinations.s3

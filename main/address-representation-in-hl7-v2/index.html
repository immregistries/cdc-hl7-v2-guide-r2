4.5.3.1 Address Representation in HL7 v2

Patient address information is carried in PID-11. HL7 v2 allows multiple address repetitions, each qualified by an address type. IIS must correctly interpret these repetitions rather than relying on positional assumptions that appeared in older guidance.

Addresses associated with contact persons may also appear in NK1 segments. These addresses serve a different purpose and are not a substitute for patient address data.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSystems SHALL send patient address information in PID-11.s1
  2. SHALLSystems SHALL use PID-11.7 (Address Type) to identify the type of address.s2
  3. SHALLIIS SHALL correctly interpret repeated PID-11 segments.s3
  4. MAYIIS MAY choose whether to retain one or multiple patient addresses.s4
  5. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT require "self" NK1 entries duplicating PID-11 content.s5
  6. SHALLIIS SHALL accept valid non-U.S. addresses when submitted.s6

2.5.1 Purpose of Acknowledgements

Acknowledgements (ACKs) are feedback tools. They inform the sending system whether its message reached the IIS’s HL7 interface, whether it was processable, and whether any problems were identified with the data itself. Every message received by an IIS receives a response, positive or negative, so that submitting systems have confirmation of delivery and processing outcome. Failure to respond leaves senders uncertain whether the IIS has accepted, rejected, or even seen their data.

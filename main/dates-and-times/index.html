2.8 Dates and Times

Accurate representation of dates and times is essential for patient identification, record matching, and correct interpretation of immunization events. HL7 v2 timestamps support both date/time precision and time zone offsets, but inconsistent use can introduce matching errors, duplicate records, and incorrect interpretation of vaccination history. This section establishes consistent expectations for how dates and times are represented in immunization messaging.

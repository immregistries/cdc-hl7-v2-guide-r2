4.7.3 Risk-Based Recommendations

In addition to age-based recommendations, jurisdictions may generate CDS output for vaccinations recommended only under specific clinical risk conditions, including risk-based recommendations, where eligibility depends on a documented risk factor, exposure, or clinical condition rather than age alone, and Shared Clinical Decision Making (SCDM) recommendations, where a vaccination is not universally recommended for everyone in an age group but may be appropriate based on individual risk and patient preference following clinical discussion. Support for these recommendations varies widely among jurisdictions and depends on local policy, the CDS engine's capabilities, and, critically, the availability of patient risk or condition data.

Risk-based recommendations require that the registry have the relevant patient risk indications; jurisdictions without access to this data may be unable to generate these recommendations regardless of CDS engine capability.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYJurisdictions MAY include recommendations for risk-based antigens, for example, HPV in adults where the routine recommendation primarily targets younger populations, or rabies vaccination for documented exposure or travel risk.s1
  2. MAYJurisdictions MAY include SCDM recommendations, for example, meningococcal B vaccination for adolescents and young adults, which is not universally recommended for everyone in the age group and is instead based on clinical discussion of individual risk and preferences.s2

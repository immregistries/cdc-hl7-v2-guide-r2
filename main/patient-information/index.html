Immunization Information Systems (IIS) receive patient information to fulfill their core responsibility: maintaining a complete, longitudinal immunization record for each individual in their jurisdiction. Because IIS aggregate data from many independent sources over time, they must continuously determine whether an incoming submission refers to an existing person or represents a new individual. Patient information therefore plays a central role in record matching and consolidation, even when individual data elements are incomplete or vary across sources.

Beyond matching, patient information supports additional IIS operational and public health functions. Date of birth enables age-based vaccine evaluation and forecasting; address information supports jurisdictional attribution and identification of geographic pockets of need; phone and email may be used for patient or guardian contact; race and ethnicity support population-level monitoring and identification of coverage gaps; and consent, privacy, and patient status inform how data may be stored, shared, and interpreted. The sections below provide context and guidance for these data elements, clarifying their intended use and expectations beyond basic HL7 v2 structural requirements.

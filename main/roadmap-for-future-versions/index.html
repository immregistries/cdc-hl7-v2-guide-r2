6.3 Roadmap for Future Versions

The HL7 v2.5.1 Implementation Guide for Immunization Messaging has been updated based on lessons learned over the past decade, incorporating many community-identified updates. However, not all proposed changes could be included in this release. Larger topics, such as consent management and clinical trial reporting, were deferred due to time constraints. As such, this guide should be seen as an iterative update rather than a final product. Future versions will incorporate additional functionality and improvements, ensuring the guide evolves alongside community needs and technological advancements. Work on the next version will begin as this guide nears completion, with no specific timeline currently set.

In addition to periodic updates to this guide, new standards outside of HL7 v2 may be developed to address emerging needs. For example, the development of new FHIR Implementation Guides is already underway to support additional functionality. Non-immunization-related data, such as occupation, immigration status, and other medical or programmatic details, may not fit well within the scope of this guide. Instead, such data may be more appropriately handled in separate standards tailored to those specific needs. This guide remains focused on its primary mission: supporting the accurate reporting and querying of immunization data to build a complete immunization history.

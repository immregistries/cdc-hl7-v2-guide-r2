4.5.3.5 County Information

Quality review 2 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note Statement s3 Its subject is "It", not a system or party, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-cee9585b
  • Note 4 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3, s4.qf-statement-from-paragraph-b7337a45

County information is carried in XAD.9 but is difficult for submitting systems to populate consistently. Multiple coding systems exist, and values are often missing or incorrect. IIS should treat county as a derived attribute rather than a submission requirement.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT require submitters to include county codes.s1
  2. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT reject addresses with missing or invalid county values.s2
  3. SHOULDIf county information is submitted, it SHOULD use the five-digit FIPS county code.⚑s3
  4. SHOULDIIS SHOULD generate or validate county information internally using a third-party address cleansing service, since submitters are not required to provide it.s4

Over time, vaccine products are discontinued, replaced, or updated, and corresponding CVX codes may be marked as inactive or retired. In most cases, retirement indicates that a vaccine product is no longer valid for administration in the United States, not that previously recorded vaccinations are invalid or should be removed. Historical vaccination records that reference retired CVX codes remain clinically meaningful and must be preserved.

In practice, there is often a lag between when a product is no longer available for ordering and when it is no longer administered. Products may remain in circulation for a period of time after retirement, and submitting systems may continue to report administered vaccinations using retired CVX codes that accurately reflect what was given. IIS and submitting systems must therefore balance two competing needs: preserving accurate historical data while ensuring that current workflows transition away from retired codes for new administrations.

Acceptance of Post-Retirement Submissions

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL preserve CVX codes recorded in patient immunization histories, even after those codes are retired or become inactive.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL treat vaccinations reported with retired CVX codes as valid historical records when they accurately represent doses administered.s2
  3. SHALLIIS SHALL continue to return retired CVX codes in RSP messages when they are part of a patient's immunization history.s3
  4. SHALLIIS SHALL accept vaccination records reported with retired CVX codes when the vaccination was administered prior to retirement.s4
  5. SHOULDIIS SHOULD be prepared to accept administered vaccination reports using retired CVX codes during transition periods when products remain in circulation.s5
  6. SHOULDIIS SHOULD monitor use of retired CVX codes for administered vaccinations and coordinate with submitting systems to transition to appropriate replacement codes.s6
  7. SHOULDIIS SHOULD distinguish between historical use of retired codes and inappropriate continued use for new administrations as part of data quality monitoring.s7

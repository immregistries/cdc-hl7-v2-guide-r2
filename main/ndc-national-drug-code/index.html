4.6.2.3 NDC - National Drug Code

The National Drug Code (NDC) is maintained by the FDA in collaboration with manufacturers and is designed to support drug manufacturing, distribution, and product identification. In immunization workflows, NDC identifies the specific vaccine product and packaging associated with an administered dose. While NDC provides important product-level detail and traceability, it is not intended to serve as the primary representation of a patient's immunization history.

NDCs are expected to change over time as products are introduced, reformulated, repackaged, or retired. This cycling of NDC values reflects the needs of the pharmaceutical supply chain rather than long-term clinical documentation. In contrast, CVX is designed to persist across the lifetime of the patient and to support longitudinal immunization histories, evaluation, and forecasting. For this reason, NDC complements CVX but does not replace it.

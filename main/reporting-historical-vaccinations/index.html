4.6.2.5 Reporting Historical Vaccinations

Quality review 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note 4 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3, s4.qf-statement-from-paragraph-9f38afa2

Historical vaccinations are doses that were administered outside the submitting system and later recorded based on available documentation or patient history. The requirements for reporting historical vaccinations largely mirror those for administered vaccinations, with additional flexibility to support legacy, foreign, and incomplete records.

All requirements specified in Vaccination Record Update (VXU) messages apply equally to historical vaccinations, except as explicitly noted below.

Additional CVX Requirements for Historical Vaccinations

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL accept Inactive CVX codes for historical vaccinations.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL accept Non-US CVX codes for historical vaccinations.s2
  3. SHOULDSubmitting systems SHOULD use the most specific CVX code available that accurately represents the historical vaccination.s3
  4. MAYSubmitting systems MAY use category-level or unspecified formulation CVX codes when the exact formulation cannot be determined.s4

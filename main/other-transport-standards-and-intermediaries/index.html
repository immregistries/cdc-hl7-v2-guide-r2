While the CDC WSDL is the required national standard, IIS implementers may also support other transport methods (e.g., HIE gateways or proprietary APIs). However, these must not supersede or exclude the CDC WSDL interface. The purpose of this requirement is to ensure that providers and systems have a consistent, non-proprietary option for connection to any IIS.

This requirement applies to the transport capability an IIS makes available to its trading partners, regardless of the underlying software system or organizational structure used to deliver it. Jurisdictions may implement this capability directly or through intermediaries such as Health Information Exchanges or other gateway services. However, whatever system functions as the entry point for message submission and query must support the CDC WSDL requirements defined in this section.

If an IIS is accessible only through an intermediary such as a Health Information Exchange (HIE), that intermediary is functionally acting as the IIS transport layer and therefore SHALL conform to all requirements in this section (Section 2.4), including full CDC WSDL support. The HIE may offer additional transport options or preferred local interfaces, but it SHALL NOT restrict submitters or querying systems from using the CDC WSDL if they choose to do so.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYIIS MAY implement additional transport standards at their discretion.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL permit any submitter to use the CDC WSDL for message exchange.s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY continue to support existing transport methods indefinitely, provided they do not exclude CDC WSDL access.s3
  4. SHALLWhen an intermediary such as an HIE is the sole access point to the IIS, that intermediary SHALL meet all CDC WSDL transport requirements defined for IIS.s4

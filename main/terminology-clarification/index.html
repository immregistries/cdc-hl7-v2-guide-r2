4.8.3 Terminology Clarification

The term contraindication is used inconsistently and is a common source of confusion.

In this guide:

Patient characteristics are treated as conditions.

Decisions not to administer a specific dose are treated as dose-level non-administration.

Use of the unqualified term contraindication is discouraged.

This separation avoids conflating patient context with encounter-level decisions and aligns with CDS-oriented models.

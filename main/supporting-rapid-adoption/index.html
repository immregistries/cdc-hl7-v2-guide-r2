While the update process ensures careful vetting, the community must also be prepared for situations requiring rapid implementation. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, swift changes were necessary. In such cases, TABs provide a mechanism for rapid dissemination of guidance, allowing the community to act while longer-term vetting and formalization occur. This approach ensures that urgent needs are addressed while maintaining a clear pathway for community feedback and refinement.

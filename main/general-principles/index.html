4.5.4.1 General Principles

Quality review 3 warnings · 6 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning Statement s2 TAB Messaging Phone and Email; Telecommunication Use Code and Equipment Type Values (Executive Summary; PID-13 Field Definition (Table 2)): Partly covered. The guide's statements cover PID-13 only (s2: "The first repetition of PID-13 SHALL represent the primary contact number"). The narrative mentions NK1-5; QPD-9 is not mentioned anywhere in 4.5.4.
    Partly coveredAction: Narrative
    TAB The primary phone number, if known, shall be in the first repetition of PID-13, QPD-9, or NK1-5, with a use code from HL70201 and an equipment type from HL70202. Each type of telecommunication shall be in its own repetition; if the primary number is not sent, the first repetition is empty.
    Recommendation Make s2 and s4 apply to PID-13, NK1-5, and QPD-9.

  • Warning Statement s1 TAB Messaging Phone and Email; Telecommunication Use Code and Equipment Type Values (PID-13 Field Definition (Table 2, clarification)): Conflict. The guide says "Systems SHALL send patient contact information in PID-13 and guardian or next-of-kin contact information in NK1-5", which reads as keeping a guardian's number out of PID-13. Its narrative (a newborn's number may belong to a parent) and s5 agree with the TAB, so the statement is the problem.
    ConflictAction: Narrative
    TAB PID-13 is intended to support contact with the patient, and may hold the contact information of the patient or the patient's guardian; for a minor, the guardian's contact information should be here, and may also be in NK1-5.
    Recommendation Reword s1: "PID-13 SHALL carry the contact information used to reach the patient, which for a minor may be a guardian's. Next-of-kin and guardian contact information SHALL be sent in NK1-5."

    See the TAB comparison

  • Warning TAB Messaging Phone and Email; Telecommunication Use Code and Equipment Type Values (QPD-9 compared with IGAMT): Conflict. In IGAMT, QPD-9 (Patient Home Phone) is 0..1 in the Z34 and Z44 queries, so a query cannot carry a phone number and an email. The TAB's rule cannot be followed for QPD-9 as profiled.
    ConflictAction: CDC decision
    TAB The email goes in a later repetition of QPD-9 (as for PID-13 and NK1-5).
    Recommendation Decide whether QPD-9 should repeat (an IGAMT change) or whether a query carries only the primary phone, and say so in 4.5.4.

    qf-tab-pe-g01

Each repetition of PID-13 or NK1-5 represents a distinct contact channel. The first repetition is used to convey the primary contact method, typically a phone number. Additional repetitions may convey alternate phone numbers or an email address. When an email address is present without an accompanying phone number, the primary repetition is intentionally left empty to preserve positional meaning.

IIS may receive contact information for either the patient or a guardian, depending on age and workflow context. Both are acceptable and expected.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSystems SHALL send patient contact information in PID-13 and guardian or next-of-kin contact information in NK1-5.⚑s1
  2. SHALLThe first repetition of PID-13 SHALL represent the primary contact number.⚑s2
  3. SHALLEach contact method (phone or email) SHALL be sent in its own repetition.⚑s3
  4. SHALLIf no primary phone number is available, systems SHALL include an empty first repetition before the email repetition.s4
  5. SHALLIIS SHALL accept and store contact information for either the patient or guardian, as appropriate.⚑s5

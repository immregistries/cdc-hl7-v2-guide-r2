4.5.4.1 General Principles

Each repetition of PID-13 or NK1-5 represents a distinct contact channel. The first repetition is used to convey the primary contact method, typically a phone number. Additional repetitions may convey alternate phone numbers or an email address. When an email address is present without an accompanying phone number, the primary repetition is intentionally left empty to preserve positional meaning.

IIS may receive contact information for either the patient or a guardian, depending on age and workflow context. Both are acceptable and expected.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSystems SHALL send patient contact information in PID-13 and guardian or next-of-kin contact information in NK1-5.s1
  2. SHALLThe first repetition of PID-13 SHALL represent the primary contact number.⚑s2
  3. SHALLEach contact method (phone or email) SHALL be sent in its own repetition.⚑s3
  4. SHALLIf no primary phone number is available, systems SHALL include an empty first repetition before the email repetition.s4
  5. SHALLIIS SHALL accept and store contact information for either the patient or guardian, as appropriate.s5

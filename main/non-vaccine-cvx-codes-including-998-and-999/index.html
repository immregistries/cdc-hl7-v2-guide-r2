The CVX code set includes a small number of codes that do not represent vaccines. These non-vaccine CVX codes exist for two primary reasons. Historically, IIS sometimes served as the only longitudinal public health record for childhood care, and certain non-vaccine concepts were added to support public health objectives similar to vaccination tracking. More recently, new products have emerged that provide protection against vaccine-preventable diseases but are not themselves vaccines and interact with immunization workflows.

Definition and Use of Non-Vaccine CVX Codes

Non-vaccine CVX codes represent products or concepts such as immune globulins, tuberculin skin tests, or other non-vaccine interventions.

These codes are not immunizations and do not represent vaccine doses for evaluation or forecasting purposes.

IIS are not required to accept, store, or return non-vaccine CVX codes in immunization histories.

Structural Use of CVX 998

CVX 998 is used to satisfy specific HL7 structural requirements where an RXA segment is required but no vaccine is being reported.

Prohibition of CVX 999

CVX 999 has no defined operational use in this implementation guide.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYIIS MAY support other non-vaccine CVX codes according to local policy.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL support CVX 998 only for the structural HL7 use cases defined in this guide.s2
  3. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT treat CVX 998 as an administered, historical, or refused vaccination in the patient's immunization history.s3
  4. SHALL NOTSubmitting systems SHALL NOT use CVX 998 to report administered, historical, or refused vaccinations.s4
  5. SHALL NOTIIS and submitting systems SHALL NOT use CVX 999 for immunization reporting.s5
  6. SHALLIIS SHALL treat the presence of CVX 999 in RXA-5 as an error condition.s6

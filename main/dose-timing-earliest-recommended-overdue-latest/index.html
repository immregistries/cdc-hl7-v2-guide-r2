4.7.8.5 Dose Timing (Earliest, Recommended, Overdue, Latest)

Forecast dates communicate when a dose may be given, when it should ideally be given, and, in optional cases, when it becomes overdue or when administration is no longer recommended. Earliest and recommended dates express the clinically relevant window for administration. Overdue and latest dates may be provided but vary in definition across CDS implementations: the meaning of an overdue date often requires discussion with the registry, while a latest date may reflect a schedule's maximum-age constraint for certain vaccines and can be authoritative for those vaccines.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLAn earliest date SHALL be returned when another dose is indicated.⚑s1
  2. SHALLA recommended date SHALL be returned when another dose is indicated.⚑s2
  3. MAYAn overdue date MAY be returned; interpretation depends on local CDS/jurisdictional definitions and should be discussed with the registry when interpretation matters.⚑s3
  4. MAYA latest date MAY be returned; for vaccines that define a maximum age (for example, rotavirus), the latest date may be authoritative, while for others it may have limited applicability.⚑s4

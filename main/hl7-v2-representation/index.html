  • Issue Patient conditions: the text uses 30945-0 in the no-vaccination group; the profiles only allow 75323-6 there. Section 4.8.6.2 says conditions are sent as 30945-0 (contraindication/precaution) observations in an ORC/RXA group that reports no vaccination. Section 4.4.4.4 and Figure 20 say patient conditions use 75323-6 in the Patient Observations group. The co-constraint tables follow 4.4.4.4: in the no-vaccination (CVX 998) group, 30945-0 is not allowed, and 75323-6 (with 85585-8 and 88878-4) is. 30945-0 is only allowed in the contraindicated group (NA with a real CVX).
    ConflictAction: Narrative and IGAMT
    Narrative 4.8.6.2: "conditions are expressed using existing vaccination contraindication/precaution LOINC codes … 30945-0 … REQUIRED to convey the condition itself"; 4.8.6.3: "Submitting systems SHALL include LOINC 30945-0 with a valid SNOMED CT value to convey a condition."
    IGAMT export
    • Z22 no-vaccination table (RXA-20 NA, CVX 998, RXA-6 999): 31044-1, 75505-8, 59784-9, group 75323-6/85585-8/88878-4.
    • Z22 contraindicated table (RXA-20 NA, CVX ≠ 998): group 30945-0/30946-8/30944-3/48767-8.
    Recommendation CDC decides which representation is current, then aligns sections 4.4.4.4 and 4.8.6, Figure 20, and the co-constraint tables.

Patient-level conditions are reported as observations using a single ORC/RXA group that explicitly indicates that no vaccination event is being reported. This ORC/RXA group functions solely as a container for OBX segments conveying clinical meaning.

On the wire, conditions are expressed using existing vaccination contraindication/precaution LOINC codes, even though the clinical concept being conveyed is broader than a simple contraindication.

The following LOINC codes are used:

30945-0 - Vaccination contraindication/precaution

REQUIRED to convey the condition itself

30946-8 - Vaccination contraindication/precaution effective date

OPTIONAL

Indicates when the condition became applicable

30944-3 - Vaccination temporary contraindication/precaution expiration date

OPTIONAL

Indicates when a temporary condition is no longer applicable

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLOBX-5 SHALL contain a SNOMED CT code from the PHVS_VaccinationContraindication_IIS value set⚑s1
  2. SHALLMultiple conditions SHALL be sent as separate OBX segments within the same ORC/RXA group.⚑s2

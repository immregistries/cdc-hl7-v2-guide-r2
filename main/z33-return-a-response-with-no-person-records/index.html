3.5.2.4 Z33 - Return a Response with No Person Records

Quality review 1 warning

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning Example Example "Consent/Disclosure Restriction Flow (Z44 → Z33)" (message 1): MSH-4 (Sending Facility) is required in MSH_#ConsolidatedIG_IZ01 but empty.qf-example-required-42732c7b

A Z33 response indicates that the IIS cannot return a patient-level immunization record for the query as submitted. This may occur when no matching records are found, when potential matches exist but IIS policy prohibits returning them, or when a matching patient record exists but cannot be disclosed due to consent or sharing restrictions.

In all cases, Z33 communicates that the IIS has processed the query but cannot provide patient-level immunization information. No follow-up query can resolve the request unless additional or different identifying information becomes available.

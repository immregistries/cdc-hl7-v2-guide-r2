5.2 Role of Certification in Immunization Messaging

Certification establishes that a system or function has the capability to exchange immunization data using nationally defined standards. Certification evaluates software behavior under defined conditions; it does not guarantee that all messages exchanged in production will conform to the guide.

Certified functions or capabilities are expected to support consistent functionality, but real-world operations introduce variability. Differences in configuration, data availability, workflow, and partner readiness mean that certification alone is insufficient to ensure ongoing data quality. Additional operational monitoring and conformance assessment are required. See Section 7, Message Infrastructure, and the applicable ONC certification criteria for the detailed functional requirements and test artifacts associated with certified functions.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTCertification of functions or capabilities SHALL NOT be interpreted as evidence that all production messages fully conform to this guide.⚑s1
  2. SHOULDIIS and submitting systems SHOULD implement post-certification monitoring and conformance assessment processes to evaluate ongoing data quality and to detect production-level nonconformance.s2

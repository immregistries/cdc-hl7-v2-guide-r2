4.6.1.2 Historical Vaccinations

Quality review 5 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors. Not part of the guide.

  • Note MIROW guide Vaccination Level Deduplication in Immunization Information Systems (P10, Table 9 - Confidence in the data): Partly covered. The message carries this information: RXA-9 distinguishes a new administration (00) from historical records by source (from another provider, another registry, a written record, a school record, a birth certificate, a public agency, or the patient's recall, which the value set marks as low reliability). The narrative never mentions these codes or asks submitters to send the most specific one rather than 01 (source unspecified).
    Partly coveredAction: Narrative
    Best practice The confidence placed in a record depends on who submitted it and how: a primary submitter reporting administered doses from a clinical system ranks highest, claims data lower, and secondary submitters (historical records) from medium to low, in an order each IIS sets (for example, schools with nurses rank higher).
    Recommendation In Historical Vaccinations, list the RXA-9 historical source codes, ask submitters to send the most specific source they know, and note that IIS use it to weigh records when deduplicating (MIROW P10).

A historical vaccination is one that the reporting organization did not administer. These records are derived from secondhand sources such as patient recall, vaccine cards, or records from other providers.

Historical reporting exists to support construction of a complete vaccination history, particularly for patients whose records pre-date electronic capture or span multiple care settings. These records are essential for evaluation and forecasting, even when product-level detail is limited.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLHistorical vaccinations SHALL represent doses not administered by the reporting organization.⚑s1
  2. MAYHistorical records MAY omit product-level details that cannot reasonably be determined.⚑s2
  3. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT decrement inventory for historical vaccinations.s3
  4. SHOULDIIS SHOULD expect duplicate reporting of historical doses from multiple sources.s4
  5. MAYHistorical vaccinations MAY include any of the data elements typically used for administered vaccinations when available (e.g., vaccine code (CVX/NDC), administration date, administering provider, lot number, manufacturer, route/site of administration, funding/eligibility indicators, etc.).⚑s5

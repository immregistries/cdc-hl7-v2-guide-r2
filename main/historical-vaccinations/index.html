4.6.1.2 Historical Vaccinations

A historical vaccination is one that the reporting organization did not administer. These records are derived from secondhand sources such as patient recall, vaccine cards, or records from other providers.

Historical reporting exists to support construction of a complete vaccination history, particularly for patients whose records pre-date electronic capture or span multiple care settings. These records are essential for evaluation and forecasting, even when product-level detail is limited.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLHistorical vaccinations SHALL represent doses not administered by the reporting organization.⚑s1
  2. MAYHistorical records MAY omit product-level details that cannot reasonably be determined.⚑s2
  3. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT decrement inventory for historical vaccinations.s3
  4. SHOULDIIS SHOULD expect duplicate reporting of historical doses from multiple sources.s4
  5. MAYHistorical vaccinations MAY include any of the data elements typically used for administered vaccinations when available (e.g., vaccine code (CVX/NDC), administration date, administering provider, lot number, manufacturer, route/site of administration, funding/eligibility indicators, etc.).⚑s5

Validation must distinguish between technical correctness and program policy enforcement. From a messaging perspective, the priority is to capture what actually happened. From a program perspective, jurisdictions may require stricter behavior from organizations receiving publicly funded vaccines, as long as they ensure that data can be corrected and resubmitted.

"Borrowing" and "payback" scenarios-where private stock is used for a publicly eligible patient or vice versa-are governed by program policy and vary by jurisdiction. Messages must reflect the actual inventory used and the eligibility at the time of vaccination, even when borrowing occurs.

Validation of funding eligibility and funding source SHOULD confirm that the codes used are valid and appropriate for the LOINC specified in OBX-3 (for example, only funding source codes for the funding source LOINC), especially for participants in publicly funded vaccine programs.

Conformance statements

  1. SHOULDIIS SHOULD avoid using HL7 v2 conformance rules to enforce all aspects of funding program policy and SHOULD instead rely on data quality processes and program operations to address unexpected or noncompliant combinations.⚑s1
  2. MAYJurisdictions MAY impose stricter funding eligibility and funding source requirements on organizations participating in publicly funded vaccine programs when necessary to support inventory decrementing and program oversight, provided they have processes to ensure that rejected records can be corrected and resubmitted.s2
  3. SHALLIn all cases, submitting systems SHALL report the funding eligibility and funding source that actually applied at the time of vaccination, including in borrowing/payback scenarios, regardless of whether the underlying practice fully complied with program policy.s3
  4. MAYWhen a funding source or eligibility code is unrecognized or inconsistent with the jurisdiction's selected stock model, IIS MAY accept the administered vaccination while flagging the issue for data quality review rather than rejecting the record.s4

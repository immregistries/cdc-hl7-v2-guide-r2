2.5.6 Shared Responsibility

Data quality is a shared obligation. Submitters rely on IIS to provide accurate immunization histories, while IIS rely on submitters to provide complete and well-formed data. Acknowledgements represent one element of this partnership, serving as structured feedback that helps both parties understand how a submitted message was processed and whether corrections may be needed.

This feedback is most effective when it reflects issues that the IIS genuinely expects submitters to review or correct, rather than conditions known to be inconsequential or routinely disregarded. When acknowledgements consistently report warnings or errors that don't require action, submitters may begin to disregard acknowledgement feedback altogether, undermining its usefulness as a data-quality communication tool. Acknowledgements work best when the issues they report accurately reflect an IIS's actual data-quality standards and operational priorities.

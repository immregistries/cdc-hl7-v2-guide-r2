4.8.6.1 Patient-Level Conditions

Patient-level conditions represent ongoing or point-in-time facts about the patient. They exist independently of a specific vaccination encounter and may influence immunization decisions across multiple visits or vaccines. A single condition may contraindicate some vaccines while indicating others.

Examples include:

Egg allergy

Pregnancy

Immunocompromised state

Chronic medical conditions affecting vaccine suitability

These observations do not assert that a vaccine was offered, withheld, or refused. They provide clinical context and are a primary input to clinical decision support logic.

2.2 Immunization Systems Process Overview

Immunization data exchange relies on interoperability between Immunization Information Systems (IIS) and a variety of external healthcare IT systems, referred to in this guide as submitting/querying systems. These systems collect, manage, and transmit vaccination data to IIS and request complete immunization histories and forecasts in return. While many of these systems are Electronic Health Records (EHRs), this guide uses the broader term submitting/querying system to encompass any system involved in these functions, including pharmacy systems, hospital information systems, school information systems, and other health-related applications.

4.4.4 Order Segment Group Flavors

Quality review 1 warning

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning The Order Segment Group flavors (text and Figure 20) and the co-constraint tables do not match. The flavor sections and Figure 20 list which observations belong in each Order Segment Group. The co-constraint tables allow more in some places and less in others. Main differences: serological immunity, history of disease, and patient condition are allowed in the administered and historical event groups (the text puts them only in Patient Observations). In Z32 they are allowed in every group. The contraindication set is required in Figure 20 but optional in the tables. Indication observations are missing from the historical-event table. Z32, the history-only response, has evaluation and forecast groups. Z42 has a single table with no conditions (see the Z42 finding). The cell-by-cell comparison is on the comparison page.
    ConflictAction: Narrative and IGAMTAnswers Word comment 31
    Narrative Sections 4.4.4.1–4.4.4.6 and Figure 20. Comments 30 and 31: "Confirm that the co-constraint tables address this section"; "Make sure these are in alignment with the Co-constraint tables".
    IGAMT export
    • Z22 administered table: also allows 75505-8, 59784-9, 75323-6 group; 31044-1 appears twice.
    • Z22 contraindicated table: contraindication group usage S [0..*].
    • Z32 table 5 (no condition) allows 31044-1, 75505-8, 59784-9, 75323-6 in every group.
    • Z32 tables 1 and 2 contain Immunization Forecast Results and Immunization Evaluation Results groups.
    Recommendation Agree with CDC which is intended, then align both. The text and Figure 20 are the more restrictive and readable statement, so the tables probably need to change. Once aligned, generate the figure from the co-constraint tables so they cannot drift apart again.

The sections below describe each Order Segment Group flavor, its purpose, and the types of observations expected within it. These groupings define where specific LOINC-coded OBX segments are meaningful. Later sections of this guide provide detailed semantic and conformance requirements, particularly for clinical decision support.

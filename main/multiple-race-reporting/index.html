4.5.5.3 Multiple Race Reporting

Patients may identify with more than one race. HL7 v2 supports this by allowing PID-10 to repeat. Each race selected by the patient is reported independently; there is no special code for "multi-racial."

The "Other race" code represents a distinct selection and does not indicate multiple races by itself.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIf a patient reports multiple races, each SHALL be sent as a separate repetition of PID-10.⚑s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL support and process multiple PID-10 repetitions.s2
  3. SHALL"Other race" (2131-1) SHALL only be sent if explicitly selected by the patient.⚑s3
  4. MAYIf no race information is provided, PID-10 MAY be omitted or populated with UNK, ASKU, or PHC1175.⚑s4

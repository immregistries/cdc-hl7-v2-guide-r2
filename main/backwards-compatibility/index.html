6.1.3 Backwards Compatibility

Although the updated guide introduces new requirements and improvements, it is designed with attention to backwards compatibility. The goal is to enable systems to implement new functionalities while continuing to interoperate with older implementations. This approach allows systems to gracefully handle interactions with partners that have not yet adopted the latest guidance, ensuring continuity in data exchange and minimizing disruptions. Additionally, systems adopting the new version should maintain pathways for older systems to upgrade incrementally as resources and policies allow.

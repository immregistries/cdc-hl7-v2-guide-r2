5.11.4 Adaptation of Interaction Patterns and Acknowledgements

IIS workflows require meaningful, actionable acknowledgements that communicate final acceptance or rejection, not just transport-level receipt. The broader v2 community does not have consensus on ACK semantics and is unlikely to converge due to legacy implementations and low active participation. IIS implementers have continued to refine ACK usage, leading to patterns that would likely be adopted broadly if v2 innovation were still active.

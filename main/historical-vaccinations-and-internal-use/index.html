4.6.5.6 Historical Vaccinations and Internal Use

Quality review 2 warnings · 4 notes

  • Note Statement s1 Statement repeats its conformance verb, so it may hold more than one requirement. Consider splitting it.qf-compound-statement-0088e457
  • Note Statement s1 Its subject is "These values", not a system or party, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-b0cf9226
  • Note 3 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3.qf-statement-from-paragraph-b44552e2
  • Note In the draft, explanatory text continues after the conformance statements. Cards show statements after the narrative, so check that the order still reads well.qf-statements-mid-section-526e28b5

Funding eligibility and funding source may appear on historical vaccinations when older doses are being reclassified, or when a submitting system sends a full record that includes both administered and historical doses.

Funding eligibility and funding source are not intended for sharing in query responses. They are operational artifacts, not part of the clinically relevant immunization history shared with other organizations, and IIS SHALL NOT return funding eligibility or funding source in RSP messages.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYThese values MAY be sent on historical vaccinations, and IIS use them, when relevant, for inventory and program operations; IIS are not required to store these values for historical doses, though they MAY retain them for audit or reconciliation purposes.⚑s1
  2. SHOULD NOTIIS SHOULD NOT use funding eligibility or funding source on historical doses to decrement inventory or drive program operations, and MAY ignore them when not needed.⚑s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY normalize, correct, or update funding eligibility and funding source values internally over time to align with program operations, without attempting to preserve those changes as part of the clinical patient history returned to external systems.s3

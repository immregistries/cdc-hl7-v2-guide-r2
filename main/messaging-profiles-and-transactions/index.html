3 Messaging Profiles and Transactions

This section describes the primary messaging profiles and transactions supported by this guide and how they relate to one another. It provides a high-level view of the overall exchange model, clarifying what interactions are in scope and how the pieces fit together. Detailed operational guidance and technical specifications for these profiles are covered in Sections 4, Data Standardization and Integration, and 7, Message Infrastructure.

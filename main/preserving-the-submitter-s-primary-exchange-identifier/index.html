4.5.1.4 Preserving the Submitter's Primary Exchange Identifier

The IIS retains the submitter's current primary exchange identifier as the linkage between the submitter's local patient record and the consolidated IIS patient record. The identifier value, identifier type, and assigning authority are preserved so the identifier can be returned accurately to the same submitting system.

Preservation does not require the IIS to use assigning authority in patient matching or as part of an internal uniqueness key. An IIS may continue to segregate submitter identifiers using MSH-4, the connection context, or another established mechanism.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL retain the identifier value, identifier type, and assigning authority of the current primary exchange identifier associated with the patient record.s1

IIS vary widely in whether they store immunity information. While documenting immunity information is recognized in the CDC Core Data Elements (for example, Evidence of Immunity and Date of Immunity Evidence), its collection, storage, and operational use vary across jurisdictions and systems. Therefore, this guide does not define update or delete mechanisms for immunity observations. If incorrect immunity information is submitted, submitters must coordinate directly with the IIS to correct it. Multiple observations for the same disease may be reported without restriction.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYIIS MAY store or ignore immunity observations.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL process messages normally even if they ignore immunity observations.s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY store multiple immunity observations for the same disease.s3

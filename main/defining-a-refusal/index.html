4.8.7.1 Defining a Refusal

A refusal represents an intentional declination of a vaccination that was both due and available for administration at that encounter. It should not be used to document missed appointments, vaccine unavailability, shared decision-making deferrals, or clinical contraindications.

A VXU may contain zero administered vaccinations (vaccination cardinality [0..*]) and may be used to submit refusal information only.

Note: VXU Messages MAY contain zero vaccinations (vaccination cardinality [0 .. *] and MAY be used to submit refusal information only.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSystems SHALL record a refusal only when the vaccine was due and the patient or guardian declined administration.s1
  2. SHALL NOTSystems SHALL NOT record refusals for cases of missed appointments, vaccine shortage, contraindications, or shared decision-making not to vaccinate.s2

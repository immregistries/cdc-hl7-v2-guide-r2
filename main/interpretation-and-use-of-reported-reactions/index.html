4.8.9.2 Interpretation and Use of Reported Reactions

The presence of a reaction in an IIS does not imply that the IIS will use the information in evaluation or recommendation generation. No guidance is provided on converting reported reactions into contraindications or precautions, and IIS may use local rules for interpreting this information. Conflicts and overlaps between reactions and contraindications are not addressed by this guide. Please contact your local IIS to understand how these reactions are supported.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYIIS MAY use reported reactions in evaluation or recommendation logic according to local policy.s1

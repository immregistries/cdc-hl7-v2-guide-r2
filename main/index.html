HL7 Version 2.5.1 Implementation Guide for Immunization Messaging

A joint publication of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the American Immunization Registry Association

This is a working draft of the next national HL7 Version 2.5.1 implementation guide for immunization messaging. It is organized as short sections, each focused on one topic, with its conformance statements listed separately from the explanatory text. Every section has a permanent link.

Quality review findings are shown alongside the text. They come from automated checks and editorial review, and they are there for the authors and reviewers to consider; they are not part of the guide. Use Hide findings to read without them.

    This Implementation Guide (IG) defines the national standard for exchanging immunization data between Immunization Information Systems (IIS) and the Electronic Health Records (EHRs), pharmacy systems, school health systems, and other submitting/querying systems that report or request immunization data.

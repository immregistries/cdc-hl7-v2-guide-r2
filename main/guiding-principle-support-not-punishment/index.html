6.1.11 Guiding Principle: Support, Not Punishment

The purpose of operating under the latest standard is to enable high-quality, interoperable data exchange, not to police or punish partners. Systems should adopt a collaborative approach, working with partners to address issues while maintaining data flow and program effectiveness. By focusing on the overarching goals of data quality and public health, systems can ensure their operations remain aligned with the intent of the standard.

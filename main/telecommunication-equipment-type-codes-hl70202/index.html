4.5.4.3 Telecommunication Equipment Type Codes (HL70202)

Quality review 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note 3 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3.qf-statement-from-paragraph-aff26916

The telecommunication equipment type identifies the device associated with a contact number. In modern IIS workflows, its only meaningful distinction is whether a number is known to be cellular.

PH (Telephone) is used when the number type is unknown or unspecified.

CP (Cellular Phone) may be used when the number is known to be cellular.

Other equipment types are permitted by HL7 v2 but have no practical value for IIS operations.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSystems SHALL use PH for phone numbers of unknown type and CP when the number is known to be cellular.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL accept either PH or CP without inferring additional meaning.s2
  3. SHALLIIS SHALL ignore other equipment types if present.s3

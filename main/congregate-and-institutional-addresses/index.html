4.5.3.2 Congregate and Institutional Addresses

Quality review 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note 3 requirements were written as paragraphs in the draft and are shown here as conformance statements (wording unchanged): s1, s2, s3.qf-statement-from-paragraph-939cd94a

In congregate living environments, such as nursing homes, dormitories, or shelters, multiple patients legitimately share the same address. In these contexts, an address represents a location rather than a household and should not be interpreted as a family residence.

Standardization and reference data can help IIS recognize these patterns and avoid inappropriate record consolidation.

Conformance statements

  1. SHOULDIIS SHOULD recognize that identical addresses may legitimately correspond to multiple patients.s1
  2. SHOULDIIS SHOULD use cleansing services to standardize institutional addresses.s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY maintain reference tables of known institutions to improve matching and analysis.s3

Messaging Phone and Email; Telecommunication Use Code and Equipment Type Values

Primary phone first in PID-13, QPD-9, or NK1-5, email in a later repetition with use code NET, and the HL70201 and HL70202 values that systems shall support.

Published PDF · Copy in this repository

Summary

Section 4.5.4 (Phone and Email) follows this TAB: PRN and NET required, PH and CP for phones, other use codes optional and ignorable, an empty first repetition when there is only an email, and the advice to treat phone numbers as cellular. The differences: the guide says guardian contact goes in NK1-5, where the TAB says PID-13 may hold a guardian's contact for a minor; it states the first-repetition rule for PID-13 only, not NK1-5 or QPD-9; and it leaves out the TAB's SHOULD NOT for fax and internet equipment types. In IGAMT, ASN is not allowed although the TAB allows it, and QPD-9 cannot repeat, so the TAB's email in a later QPD-9 repetition cannot be sent.

Requirement by requirement

The TAB saysResultIn the guide

Executive Summary; PID-13 Field Definition (Table 2) p. 1, 3

shall The primary phone number, if known, shall be in the first repetition of PID-13, QPD-9, or NK1-5, with a use code from HL70201 and an equipment type from HL70202. Each type of telecommunication shall be in its own repetition; if the primary number is not sent, the first repetition is empty.		Partly covered Warning

The guide's statements cover PID-13 only (s2: "The first repetition of PID-13 SHALL represent the primary contact number"). The narrative mentions NK1-5; QPD-9 is not mentioned anywhere in 4.5.4.

Recommendation: Make s2 and s4 apply to PID-13, NK1-5, and QPD-9.

Action: Narrative

4.5.4.1 s2, 4.5.4.1 s3, 4.5.4.1 s4, 4.5.4.1 General Principles

PID-13 Field Definition (Table 2, clarification) p. 3

PID-13 is intended to support contact with the patient, and may hold the contact information of the patient or the patient's guardian; for a minor, the guardian's contact information should be here, and may also be in NK1-5.		Conflict Warning

The guide says "Systems SHALL send patient contact information in PID-13 and guardian or next-of-kin contact information in NK1-5", which reads as keeping a guardian's number out of PID-13. Its narrative (a newborn's number may belong to a parent) and s5 agree with the TAB, so the statement is the problem.

Recommendation: Reword s1: "PID-13 SHALL carry the contact information used to reach the patient, which for a minor may be a guardian's. Next-of-kin and guardian contact information SHALL be sent in NK1-5."

Action: Narrative

4.5.4.1 s1, 4.5.4.1 s5, 4.5.4 Phone and Email

Executive Summary; PID-13 Field Definition p. 1

shall An email address, if known, shall be in a later repetition of PID-13, QPD-9, or NK1-5 with use code NET; no equipment type is needed.		Covered

The guide does not say that no equipment type is needed for an email, or which XTN components carry the email (XTN-4) and the phone number (XTN-6, XTN-7); IGAMT's XTN_IZ01 predicates do.

4.5.4.2 s2, 4.5.4.1 s3, 4.5.4.1 s4

QPD-9 compared with IGAMT p. 1

The email goes in a later repetition of QPD-9 (as for PID-13 and NK1-5).		Conflict Warning

In IGAMT, QPD-9 (Patient Home Phone) is 0..1 in the Z34 and Z44 queries, so a query cannot carry a phone number and an email. The TAB's rule cannot be followed for QPD-9 as profiled.

Recommendation: Decide whether QPD-9 should repeat (an IGAMT change) or whether a query carries only the primary phone, and say so in 4.5.4.

Action: CDC decision

4.5.4.1 General Principles

Telecommunication Use Code (HL70201) Value Set (Table 3) p. 3-4

SHALL, MAY PRN (R) SHALL be used for the primary phone number, landline or cellular. NET (R) SHALL be used for an email. ORN, PRS, WPN, VHN, ASN, EMR, and BPN (P) MAY be used; IIS may ignore them and should continue to process the message.		Covered4.5.4.2 s1, 4.5.4.2 s2, 4.5.4.2 s3, 4.5.4.2 s4, 4.5.4.2 s5

Table 3 compared with the IGAMT value set p. 3-4

ASN (answering service number) is P, MAY be used.		Conflict Warning

HL70201_IZ01 marks ASN as E (not allowed), so a message using it does not conform, although the TAB and the guide (s3) allow it. The other codes match.

Recommendation: Change ASN to P in HL70201_IZ01.

Action: IGAMT

4.5.4.2 s3

Telecommunication Equipment Type (HL70202) Value Set (Table 4) p. 4

SHALL, SHOULD NOT PH (R) SHALL be used for a phone number, landline or cellular; CP (R) SHALL be used for a cellular phone. Internet, X.400, and FX (P) SHOULD NOT be used, and IIS SHALL ignore them if sent.		Partly covered Note

PH and CP, and the IIS ignoring other types, are covered. The SHOULD NOT for senders (Internet, X.400, fax) is not. IGAMT also lists TDD, TTY, MD, and BP as E, which the TAB does not mention; with s3 ("IIS SHALL ignore other equipment types"), the guide treats as ignorable what the profile treats as an error.

Recommendation: Add "Systems SHOULD NOT send equipment types Internet, X.400, or FX" to 4.5.4.3.

Action: Narrative

4.5.4.3 s1, 4.5.4.3 s2, 4.5.4.3 s3

Implications p. 5

Phone numbers need not be designated landline or cellular. Systems should not rely on the designation; third-party services can verify numbers and text capability. IIS should assume most numbers are cellular.		Covered4.5.4.4 s1, 4.5.4.4 s2, 4.5.4.4 s3

Examples p. 5

PID-13 examples: primary phone; phone and email; primary, secondary, and email for a newborn's contact; email only with an empty first repetition.		Missing Note

The guide describes these cases but has none of the examples.

Recommendation: Add the TAB's four PID-13 examples to 4.5.4.1 or appendix A.

Action: Narrative

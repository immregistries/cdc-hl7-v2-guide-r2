Returning IIS Patient Immunization Record Information in a Z32 and Z42 RSP

What an IIS shall, should, may, should not, and shall not return in a Z32 or Z42 response when a single patient is matched, for example completed immunizations but not deleted ones.

Summary

This TAB says what an IIS returns in a Z32 or Z42 for a single match (Table 1) and updates the Z32 and Z42 usage of PD1, NK1, and several PID, RXA, and ORC fields (Tables 2 and 3). The guide covers the general principles (complete, unfiltered history; return only what supports confirmation; protected patients get a Z33), but not the TAB's list. It contradicts the TAB on refusals (SHOULD NOT return, where the TAB says SHOULD), and leaves out the SHALL NOT for deleted immunizations, RXA-20 and RXA-21 'A' as required, and the CP/PA rule for the vaccination event fields. In IGAMT, the Z32 and Z42 profiles still have the usages the TAB replaced.

Requirement by requirement

The TAB saysResultIn the guide

Table 1: Patient types p. 3

SHALL, MAY A patient who is not protected (PD1-12 not 'Y') SHALL be returned; a protected patient is returned as a Z33. A deceased patient MAY be returned.		Partly covered Note

The guide says a Z33 is returned when a record "cannot be disclosed due to consent or sharing restrictions", and that PD1-12 affects responses, but does not tie the Z33 to PD1-12 = 'Y' or say whether a deceased patient is returned.

Recommendation: State in 3.5.2.4 that a patient with PD1-12 'Y' is returned as a Z33, and that a deceased patient MAY be returned.

SHALL, SHOULD Completed immunizations ('CP') SHALL be returned; partially administered immunizations ('PA') SHOULD be returned.		Partly covered Note

Covered in substance by "Vaccination history order groups SHALL be complete and unfiltered" and the Z32 description, but partially administered doses are not mentioned.

Recommendation: Add the TAB's two statements to 3.5.2.2 (Z32) and 3.5.2.5 (Z42).

4.7.4 s3, 3.5.2.2 Z32 - Return Complete Immunization History

Table 1: Immunization types (refused) p. 3

SHOULD Refused immunizations ('RE') SHOULD be returned.		Conflict Issue

The guide says "IIS SHOULD NOT return refusal records in query (RSP) responses" and "Querying systems SHALL NOT expect refusals to be returned", because refusals "have no role in clinical query/response exchanges". The TAB says the opposite. (The CDS in RSP Alignment TAB also lists refusals among the non-CDS order groups that follow the history.)

Recommendation: CDC and SISC decide. If the guide's position stands, it changes a published TAB and should say so; otherwise change 4.8.7.4 to the TAB's SHOULD.

Action: CDC decision

4.8.7.4 s1, 4.8.7.4 s2

Table 1: Immunization types (deleted) p. 3

SHALL NOT Deleted immunizations (processed with action code 'D') SHALL NOT be returned.		Missing Warning

The guide does not say that deleted immunizations are excluded from a response.

Recommendation: Add "IIS SHALL NOT return deleted immunizations" to 4.6.2.6 or 3.5.2.

Action: Narrative

4.6.2.6 Vaccinations Returned in Query Responses (RSP), 4.6.3 Vaccination Record Identity and ORC-3

Table 1: Observations associated with an immunization p. 3-4

MAY Funding program eligibility and funding source, VIS information, and an adverse reaction tied to a specific administration MAY be returned.		Reworded Note

The guide says IIS that store an adverse event tied to an immunization SHOULD return it, stronger than the TAB's MAY. It does not address returning eligibility, funding, or VIS (4.4.2.6 only mentions that they can appear in an RSP).

Recommendation: Confirm the SHOULD for adverse events, and add MAY statements for eligibility, funding, and VIS in responses.

Action: CDC decision

4.8.9.1 s5

Table 1: Observations associated with a patient p. 4

SHOULD, MAY Contraindications (vaccine-specific, and not vaccine-specific with product '998'), disease with serological evidence of immunity (75505-8), and disease with presumed immunity (59784-9) SHOULD be returned. An adverse reaction not tied to an administration MAY be returned.		Partly covered Warning

The guide says IIS that use immunity information for recommendations SHOULD return it, which is narrower than the TAB, and has no statement about returning contraindications. It makes returning patient-level reactions a SHOULD where the TAB says MAY.

3.5.2.5 Z42 - Return Evaluated Immunization History and Forecast, 3.5.2.2 Z32 - Return Complete Immunization History

Table 3: PID fields p. 5

SHOULD NOT, MAY In Z32 and Z42: mother's maiden name (PID-6) SHOULD NOT be returned; race (PID-10), address (PID-11), phone (PID-13), and ethnic group (PID-22) MAY be returned. PD1 and NK1 MAY be returned (Table 2).		In narrative only Note

The guide's disclosure section makes the same point (return only what supports confirmation; mother's maiden name may be used for matching but withheld), with no statements. It does not mention PD1 or NK1 in responses.

Recommendation: Add the TAB's field list to 3.5.3 as statements.

Action: Narrative

3.5.3 Information Disclosure Constraints, 4.5.5.5 OMB 2024 Directive and Forward Compatibility

Table 3: RXA-9, RXA-20, RXA-21 p. 5

MAY, SHALL RXA-9 (immunization information source) MAY be returned. RXA-20 (completion status) SHALL be returned. RXA-21 (action code) SHALL be returned, as 'A'.		Partly covered Warning

RXA-9 is covered (the guide prefers it empty in responses, which the TAB allows). Nothing says RXA-20 and RXA-21 are required in responses, or that RXA-21 is 'A'.

Recommendation: Add to 4.6.2.6: "In an RSP, RXA-20 SHALL be populated, and RXA-21 SHALL be 'A'."

Action: Narrative

4.6.1.5 Use of Administered vs. Historical on Output

Table 3: vaccination event fields p. 5-6

SHALL, SHALL NOT Administering provider (RXA-10), administered-at location (RXA-11), lot number (RXA-15), expiration date (RXA-16), manufacturer (RXA-17), and ordering provider (ORC-12): when RXA-20 is 'CP' or 'PA', SHALL be returned if known; when it is not, SHALL NOT be returned.		Missing Warning

4.6.2.6 Vaccinations Returned in Query Responses (RSP)

Tables 2 and 3 compared with IGAMT p. 4-6

The updated Z32 and Z42 usages in Tables 2 and 3.		Conflict Issue

The IGAMT Z32 and Z42 profiles still have the usages the TAB replaced (its "National IG Usage" columns). Z32: PD1 and NK1 RE (TAB O); PID-6, PID-10, PID-13, PID-22 RE (TAB O, PID-6 SHOULD NOT); PID-11 RE in both (TAB O); RXA-9 R for CP/PA (TAB O; see also qf-igamt-rxa9-rsp); RXA-20 RE (TAB R); RXA-21 O (TAB R, 'A'); RXA-10 and RXA-15 to RXA-17 and ORC-12 conditional on RXA-9 = '00' (TAB RE for CP/PA, X otherwise). Z42 has no PD1 or NK1 at all, and the same RXA differences.

Recommendation: Apply Tables 2 and 3 to PID_IZ01/IZ02, RXA_IZ02/IZ03, ORC_IZ01/IZ02, and the Z32 and Z42 message structures.

Action: IGAMT

3.5 Response (RSP) Profiles

Implications; Table 4 p. 6-7

IIS are not repositories of current identifying information, so jurisdictions choose which demographics to return. Immunization history is expected to be returned. Table 4: what each conformance verb means for the IIS (SHALL means the IIS must have the information to return) and for the querying system (it may depend only on SHALL items).		Partly covered Note

The first point is covered well. Table 4's reading of the verbs for responses (what a querying system can depend on) is not in the guide.

Recommendation: Add Table 4, or a sentence on what querying systems can rely on, to 3.5.3.

Action: Narrative

Technical Advisory Bulletin · September 2026

CDS in RSP Alignment

Refines how evaluations and forecasts are encoded in a Z42 response: one ORC/RXA order group for all forecasts, a constrained set of OBX codes, and use of LOINC 59783-1 to say whether a next dose is due or the series is complete.

Summary

Section 4.7 (Clinical Decision Support) is built on this TAB and follows its structure: evaluations in the vaccination history order groups, one forecast order group last, a forecast for every standard series, and the LOINC requirements of Tables 2 and 3. Three statements contradict the TAB: the guide lets an IIS omit dose validity (the TAB and IGAMT require it), recommends sending the legacy "Vaccines due next" LOINC (the TAB says SHOULD NOT, and IGAMT does not allow it), and forbids the component vaccine type LOINC only as the first forecast OBX (the TAB says SHALL NOT return it at all). The guide also leaves out the TAB's status concepts (SHALL support Complete and On schedule, SHOULD support Overdue and Too old), the requirement that forecast CVX codes be unique, the Table 4 answer codes, and the documentation of local status codes.

The TAB saysResultIn the guide

Scope (footnote 1); RSP Example p. 2, 9

MAY, SHALL Evaluation and forecast information MAY be returned in a Z32 and SHALL be returned in a Z42. If it is returned in a Z32, these specifications apply.		Partly covered Warning

The Z42 section says the response includes evaluation and forecast results, but no statement requires them, and nothing says a Z32 may carry them or that section 4.7 then applies. The IGAMT Z32 profile does allow a forecast group.

Recommendation: Add to 4.7: "A Z42 response SHALL include evaluation and forecast information. A Z32 response MAY include it; when it does, the requirements of this section apply."

SHALL The vaccination history section SHALL include all complete vaccinations on the IIS consolidated record, without filtering or exclusion, including those with no evaluation (for example, Japanese encephalitis).		Covered4.7.4 s3, 4.7.4 s1

Organization requirements: Vaccination history/evaluation p. 4

SHALL Each order group SHALL have zero or more evaluations, one for every vaccine group evaluated. All vaccinations that may complete a standard series SHALL have evaluation observations. 4.7.5 s1, 4.7.7.2 Vaccine Type and Component Identification, 4.4.4.5 Immunization Event with Evaluation

Organization requirements: Non-CDS Order Groups; Forecast p. 5

SHALL, SHOULD Non-CDS order groups (refusals, contraindications, adverse reactions, history of disease, immunities) SHALL follow the vaccination history. A forecast SHALL be included for all vaccines that may complete a standard series. All forecasts SHOULD appear after the immunization history and after other order groups.		Reworded Note

The guide makes the position of the forecast group a SHALL (the TAB says SHOULD), and states the order of the non-CDS groups only in narrative and figure 30.

Recommendation: Confirm that the guide means to raise the forecast position from SHOULD to SHALL.

4.7.4 s2, 4.7.8.1 s3, Figure 30

Evaluation observations p. 5

SHALL Evaluation information SHALL be communicated with OBX segments using the LOINC codes in Table 2, and the observations SHALL be linked by an identical value in OBX-4.		In narrative only Note

For forecasts, OBX-4 linking is a statement (4.7.8.1 s2). For evaluations, the guide says only that shared OBX-4 values group "related evaluation or recommendation observations" (4.4.2.4), with no SHALL.

Recommendation: Add to 4.7.7: "The OBX segments of each evaluated group SHALL share an OBX-4 value."

Action: Narrative

4.4.2.4 OBX-4 Observation Sub-ID, 4.4.4.5 Immunization Event with Evaluation, 4.7.7 Evaluation

Table 2: 30956-7 Vaccine type; 38890-0 Component vaccine type p. 5

SHALL, SHOULD NOT 30956-7 SHALL be returned first, before all other evaluation OBX segments, with a CVX in OBX-5. 38890-0 SHOULD NOT be returned (it is being deprecated in favor of 30956-7; IIS using it should plan a transition).		Covered Note

Covered, but these four statements sit under the second "Future work" heading (4.7.7.3) instead of 4.7.7.2, Vaccine Type and Component Identification, where they belong. Likewise the first "Future work" (4.7.7.1) holds two evaluation statements. Each "Future work" sub-heading in the Word draft has captured the statements of the section before or after it.

Recommendation: Move the statements to 4.7.7.2 and 4.7.7.4 (or make "Future work" a paragraph rather than a heading), and keep a single "Future work" section.

4.7.7.3 s1, 4.7.7.3 s2, 4.7.7.3 s3, 4.7.7.3 s4, 4.7.7.2 Vaccine Type and Component Identification

Table 2: 59781-5 Dose validity p. 5

SHALL, SHOULD Dose validity SHALL be returned, SHOULD come right after 30956-7 and before the other evaluation OBX segments, and SHALL be 'Y' if valid and 'N' if not. Only doses that are valid and count toward the final series are 'Y'; extraneous doses (CDSi) are 'N'.		Conflict Issue

The guide says "If dose validity cannot be determined or is unavailable at query time, the dose-validity OBX ... SHALL be omitted" (stated twice, in 4.7.7.4 s3 and in 4.7.7.1 "Future work" s2). The TAB requires dose validity for every evaluation, and so does IGAMT (59781-5 is R in the Z42 evaluation group). The Y/N rule matches; the order after the vaccine type is in narrative only.

Recommendation: Remove the omission rule (both copies), or take it to SISC as a change to the TAB and to IGAMT. Move the two statements in "Future work" (4.7.7.1) to the sections they belong to; that card is otherwise about future guidance.

Action: CDC decision

4.7.7.4 s3, 4.7.7.1 s2, 4.7.7.4 s2

Table 2: 30982-3 Reason for validity; optional observations p. 5-6

SHOULD, SHALL, MAY Reason for validity SHOULD be returned when the dose is 'N', and SHALL repeat in additional OBX segments for multiple reasons. Series name, total doses in series, dose number, and schedule used MAY be returned.		Covered4.7.7.4 s4, 4.7.7.4 s5, 4.7.7.5 s1, 4.7.7.5 s2, 4.7.7.5 s3, 4.7.7.5 s4

Forecast observations p. 6

SHALL The forecast order group SHALL be a single ORC/RXA pair where RXA-3 is the date of the forecast, RXA-5.1 is '998', and RXA-20 is 'NA'. The OBX types SHALL be repeated for every age-based recommendation, and linked by an identical OBX-4.		Partly covered Warning

Single group, one forecast per series, and OBX-4 linking are statements. The ORC/RXA values are described only for patient-level groups in general (4.4.1), which says RXA-3 "can be populated with the current date but does not convey clinical timing". The TAB requires RXA-3 to be the date of the forecast.

Recommendation: Add to 4.7.8.1 the TAB's rule for the forecast ORC/RXA: RXA-3 the forecast date, RXA-5.1 '998', RXA-20 'NA'.

Action: Narrative

4.7.8.1 s1, 4.7.8.1 s2, 4.7.8.1 s3, 4.4.1 Overview of LOINC and OBX Usage in Immunization Messaging

Table 3: 30956-7 Vaccine type p. 6

SHALL 30956-7 SHALL be returned first, before all other associated OBX, with a CVX in OBX-5; the CVX returned SHALL be unique for all forecasts.		Partly covered Warning

The guide does not say that each forecast's vaccine type CVX must be unique within the forecast group.

Recommendation: Add "The CVX in the vaccine type OBX SHALL be unique among the forecasts in the forecast order group."

Action: Narrative

4.7.8.2 s1, 4.7.8.2 s4

Table 3: 30979-9 Vaccines due next p. 6

SHOULD NOT 30979-9 (Vaccines due next) SHOULD NOT be returned.		Conflict Issue

The guide says "If needed to support legacy systems, IIS SHOULD include the legacy LOINC 30979-9 (Vaccines Due Next) after LOINC 30956-7", the opposite of the TAB. IGAMT agrees with the TAB: 30979-9 is not in the Z42 forecast co-constraints, so a message that includes it does not conform.

Recommendation: Replace s3 with "IIS SHOULD NOT return LOINC 30979-9 (Vaccines Due Next)."

Action: Narrative

4.7.8.2 s3, 4.7.8.2 Vaccine Type and Forecast Initiation

Table 3: 38890-0 Component vaccine type p. 6

SHALL NOT 38890-0 (Component vaccine type) SHALL NOT be returned in the forecast.		Partly covered Warning

The guide only forbids using 38890-0 "to initiate a forecast", which reads as allowing it later in the forecast group. The TAB forbids it anywhere in the forecast, as does IGAMT (not in the forecast co-constraints).

Recommendation: "LOINC 38890-0 (Component Vaccine Type) SHALL NOT be returned in the forecast order group."

Action: Narrative

4.7.8.2 s2

Table 3: 59783-1 Status in the immunization series p. 6-7

SHALL, SHOULD, MAY 59783-1 SHALL be returned, SHOULD come right after 30956-7 and before the other forecast OBX; OBX-5 SHOULD use LOINC answer codes, MAY use equivalent local codes, and MAY use both.		Covered

4.7.8.3 s1, 4.7.8.3 s3, 4.7.8.3 s4

Status in immunization series (concepts) p. 8

SHALL, SHOULD, MAY IIS SHALL support the concepts Complete and On schedule, SHOULD support Overdue and Too old, and MAY support others.		Partly covered Warning

The guide requires only that the status distinguish "complete" from "another dose due". It does not require On schedule as such, and does not mention Overdue or Too old as SHOULD.

Recommendation: State the TAB's concept levels: SHALL Complete and On schedule; SHOULD Overdue and Too old; MAY others.

Action: Narrative

SHALL IIS returning local codes SHALL document them in a Local Implementation Guide. Table 4 lists the LOINC answer codes: LA13421-5 Complete and LA13422-3 On schedule (R, SHALL), LA13423-1 Overdue and LA13424-9 Too old (P, SHOULD), LA27183-5 Immune, LA4216-3 Contraindicated, and LA4695-8 Not recommended (P, MAY).		Missing Warning

4.7.8.3 Status in Immunization Series

Table 3: reasons, dates, and optional observations p. 7

SHALL, MAY Reason for recommendation MAY be returned, more than once. Earliest and recommended dates SHALL be returned when 59783-1 says another dose should be given (On schedule or Overdue); overdue and latest dates MAY be. 4.7.8.7 s1, 4.7.8.7 s2

Implications of Adopting This Guidance; Future considerations p. 3

Meeting the SHALLs gives essential clinical support and readiness for AIRA's Measurement and Improvement (M&I) CDS testing; the SHOULDs position IIS for future expansion. Some MAYs are expected to become SHOULDs (invalid dose reason, dose numbering, validity codes, reasons for recommendations, status categories).		Covered

The guide refers to "interoperability measurement programs" without naming M&I. Its "Future work" text appears twice (4.7.7.1 and 4.7.7.3), with the second copy covering forecasts as well as evaluations.

4.7.6 Intended Use and Implementation Expectations, 4.7.7.1 Future work, 4.7.7.3 Future work, 4.7.8 Recommendation

RSP Example p. 9-12

A Z32/Z42 example with a yellow fever dose returned without evaluation, a Twinrix dose evaluated for HepA and HepB, an invalid HepA dose with a reason, and a forecast group with complete and on-schedule forecasts.		Partly covered Note

Technical Advisory Bulletin · April 2024

Additional "Unknown" Race and Ethnicity Codes

Adds UNK, ASKU, and PHC1175 to CDCREC for unknown, asked-but-unknown, and refused race and ethnicity, and requires receivers to process VXUs with or without valid race and ethnicity.

Summary

Section 4.5.5.2 (Unknown and Refused Values) adopts this TAB by name and cites it: UNK, ASKU, and PHC1175, MAY for senders, accepted by IIS, and translatable to UNK or empty. IGAMT has the three codes in both bound value sets. The differences: the TAB's SHALL NOT send both ASKU and PHC1175 is a SHOULD in the guide; the TAB requires the coding system "CDCREC" for the three codes, which the guide does not say and IGAMT contradicts (NULLFL, and CDCPHINVS for PHC1175 in race); and the general rule that a VXU is processed when a race or ethnicity code is not recognized is missing.

Executive Summary; Tables 1 and 2 p. 1, 3-4

MAY CDCREC is extended with UNK (Unknown), ASKU (Asked but unknown), and PHC1175 (Refused to answer) for race (PID-10) and ethnic group (PID-22). Submitting systems MAY send them in a VXU.		Covered4.5.5.2 s1, 4.5.5.2 s3, 4.5.5.3 s4, 4.5.5.4 s3, 4.5.5.2 Unknown and Refused Values

Tables 1 and 2 (submitting system conformance) p. 3-4

SHALL NOT Submitting systems SHALL NOT submit both ASKU and PHC1175 to a receiving system.		Conflict Warning

The guide says sending systems "SHOULD select and consistently implement either ASKU or PHC1175 ... but not both", a SHOULD where the TAB has SHALL NOT.

Recommendation: "Sending systems SHALL NOT send both ASKU and PHC1175 to the same receiving system; they SHALL choose one."

Action: Narrative

4.5.5.2 s2

Additional "unknown" race and ethnic group codes p. 3-4

SHALL In PID-10 and PID-22, component 1 is the code, component 2 the text, and component 3 the value set identifier, which SHALL be "CDCREC" for these codes (for example ASKU^asked but unknown^CDCREC).		Conflict Warning

The guide does not say which coding system the three codes use. IGAMT gives them as NULLFL (UNK, ASKU, and, for ethnicity, PHC1175) and CDCPHINVS (PHC1175 for race), so the TAB's own examples carry a coding system the profiles do not list. The value sets are open, so a validator would probably warn rather than fail.

Recommendation: Decide which coding system identifier these codes carry (the TAB says CDCREC), state it in 4.5.5.2, and make the IGAMT value sets match.

Action: CDC decision

4.5.5.2 Unknown and Refused Values, 4.5.5.1 Value Sets and Field Usage

Tables 1 and 2 (receiving system conformance) p. 3-4

SHOULD, MAY Receiving systems SHOULD process these codes. They MAY translate UNK to an empty value, and ASKU or PHC1175 to UNK or an empty value.		Reworded Note

The guide makes it a SHALL (IIS SHALL accept the codes, SHALL NOT reject messages because of them), stronger than the TAB's SHOULD. It does not mention translating UNK to an empty value.

Recommendation: Confirm that the guide means to raise acceptance from SHOULD to SHALL.

Action: CDC decision

4.5.5.2 Unknown and Refused Values

Processing a VXU with an unknown patient race and/or ethnic group p. 4

SHALL If a race or ethnicity code is not recognized, the receiving system raises an error but SHALL continue to process the message as if the field were not valued. Except where law or policy prohibits it, receiving systems SHALL process VXU messages with or without valid race and ethnicity codes.		Partly covered Warning

The guide says IIS must accommodate unknown and refused responses "without rejecting valid immunization messages", and must not reject the three unknown codes, but has no statement for a code the IIS does not recognize (an invalid code).

Recommendation: Add to 4.5.5.1: "Except where prohibited by law or policy, IIS SHALL process a VXU with or without valid race and ethnicity codes; an unrecognized code is reported in the ACK and the field treated as not valued."

Action: Narrative

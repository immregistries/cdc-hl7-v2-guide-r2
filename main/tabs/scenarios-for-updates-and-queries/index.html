Summary

Sections 3.2.5 (VXU Submission Scenarios) and 3.4.4 (Scenarios That Trigger QBP Messages) are built on this TAB, scenario by scenario, and section 3.2.4 carries its identifying and non-identifying field lists. Most rows match. The differences: the TAB says a submitting system SHALL NOT support both single-vaccination and whole-encounter reporting, where the guide says it MAY; the TAB's SHALL coordinate with the IIS before sending immunity, adverse reaction, contraindication, or refusal information is missing; the daily query limits for patient portals are a SHOULD in the guide where the TAB has SHALL; and the TAB's row for a modified legal name is not in the guide.

Findings: 1 issue 2 warnings 4 notes
Results: Conflict 1 · Missing 1 · Partly covered 2 · Reworded 2 · Covered 10 · Guide adds 1

Reviewed 2026-11-16 against the narrative draft of 4 Nov 2026 and IGAMT export of 6 Nov 2026. Recorded in quality/tabs/scenarios-for-updates-and-queries.yml.

Requirement by requirement

The TAB saysResultIn the guide

Executive Summary; Table 1: Vaccination administered during an encounter p. 1-2

SHALL, SHALL NOT A submitting system SHALL support one but SHALL NOT support both: a VXU with one vaccination, if reporting each vaccination individually, or a VXU with all vaccines given during the encounter, if reporting the whole encounter.		Conflict Issue

The guide says a submitting system SHALL support at least one approach and "MAY support both approaches if operationally required". The TAB says SHALL NOT support both.

Recommendation: CDC and SISC decide. If the guide's MAY stands, it changes a published TAB and should say so; otherwise change s2 to "A submitting system SHALL NOT support both approaches."

Action: CDC decision

3.2.3 s1, 3.2.3 s2, 3.2.5.1 s1, 3.2.5.1 s3

Table 1: Evidence of immunity, adverse reaction, contraindication, refusal or exemption p. 2

MAY, SHALL When evidence of immunity, an adverse reaction, a contraindication, or a refusal or exemption is recorded, the system MAY trigger a VXU, and MAY send it in a VXU with other vaccination information. It SHALL coordinate with the IIS to confirm that the IIS supports it.		Partly covered Warning

The guide allows sending each of these (MAY statements in 3.2.2 and 4.8), and says IIS MAY ignore them and SHOULD keep processing the rest of the message, so a submitter cannot tell from the ACK whether they were used. It has no statement that these recordings may trigger a VXU on their own, and none requiring coordination with the IIS before sending them (only for locally extended codes).

Recommendation: Add to 3.2.5: "Recording evidence of immunity, an adverse reaction, a contraindication, or a refusal MAY trigger a VXU, or the information MAY be sent with other vaccination information. Submitting systems SHALL coordinate with the IIS to confirm that it supports this information."

Action: Narrative

Table 1: Previously submitted vaccination modified p. 3

SHALL, SHALL NOT A change to an identifying field SHALL trigger a VXU delete (RXA-21 'D') followed by a VXU add (RXA-21 'A'). A change to only non-identifying fields SHALL trigger a VXU update (RXA-21 'U'). Viewing or opening a record without changes SHALL NOT trigger a VXU.		Covered

The guide's statements do not name the RXA-21 values ('D', 'A', 'U'); the section on updates and deletes (4.6.4) does.

Table 1: Vaccination record deleted p. 3

SHALL, SHALL NOT Deleting a record SHALL trigger a VXU delete with the same information sent in the previous add or update, except RXA-21 is 'D'. Deleting a record the organization did not report SHALL NOT trigger a VXU; the system SHALL contact the IIS directly to request cleanup.		Covered3.2.5.4 s1, 3.2.5.5 s1, 3.2.5.5 s2

Table 1: Patient demographic record created or modified p. 3

MAY, SHALL Creating a patient record or modifying a patient's demographics MAY trigger a VXU. The system SHALL coordinate with the IIS on acceptance and processing of VXUs with no immunization information.		Reworded Note

The guide requires coordination only before sending demographic-only VXUs "on an ongoing or routine basis"; the TAB requires it whenever a system sends them.

Recommendation: Drop "on an ongoing or routine basis" from s2, or confirm that the narrower requirement is intended.

3.2.5.7 s1

Table 1: Legal name modified p. 3

The guide has no row for a name change after the first. It falls under the general demographic MAY (tab-sc-07), but is not named.

Recommendation: Confirm the reading with the TAB authors, then add to 3.2.5.7: "When the official registry name is later changed, the submitting system MAY trigger a VXU."

Table 2: User requests patient immunization record p. 4

The guide's s3 (daily limits) is in the same user-initiated section and does not say it is for portals only, so it can be read as limiting clinician queries, which the TAB allows "as often as requested".

3.4.4.1 s1, 3.4.4.1 s2

Table 2: User requests record after reporting p. 4

MAY A user request after reporting vaccinations to the IIS MAY trigger a QBP.		Covered

The TAB lists this as user-initiated; the guide places it under system-initiated queries.

3.4.4.2 s3

Table 2: Patient or guardian portal p. 4

SHALL, SHOULD A patient or guardian request through a portal SHALL trigger a QBP. The system SHALL allow a limited number of queries per patient per day, SHALL coordinate with the IIS to define the limit, and SHALL have controls to prevent over-querying. It SHOULD allow a re-query after too many or no matches.		Reworded Warning

The trigger and re-query statements match. The limits are weakened: "Jurisdictions and implementers SHOULD agree on reasonable daily limits and controls to prevent over-querying", where the TAB has three SHALLs for portals.

Recommendation: Replace s3 with: "For patient or guardian portal queries, systems SHALL limit the number of queries per patient per day, SHALL coordinate the limit with the IIS, and SHALL have controls to prevent over-querying."

Action: Narrative

Appendix, Table 4: Non-identifying vaccination record fields p. 6

RXA-6 dose volume, RXA-15 lot number, RXA-16 expiration date, RXA-17 manufacturer, RXR-1 route, RXR-2 site, and the OBX observations for VIS (69764-9), VIS given date (29769-7), funding source (30963-3), and dose level eligibility (64994-7).		Partly covered Note

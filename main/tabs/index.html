Technical Advisory Bulletins (TABs) are published community specifications for the v2.5.1 Implementation Guide for Immunization Messaging, developed by AIRA and CDC and approved by AIRA's Standards and Interoperability Steering Committee (SISC). This guide is meant to fold them in. These pages check, requirement by requirement, that it does: nothing missed, and nothing that contradicts a TAB.

Each TAB links to its published PDF on the AIRA repository and to the copy kept with this guide's source. 12 of 12 TABs have been compared so far. Results that need attention also appear as quality findings on the guide sections they concern.

What the results mean

  • Conflict The guide or the profiles say something different.
  • Missing Not in the guide.
  • Partly covered Some of it is in the guide.
  • Reworded The guide says it differently; check that the meaning is unchanged.
  • In narrative only The guide says it, but in narrative rather than as a numbered conformance statement.
  • Covered The guide says the same thing.
  • Guide adds A requirement in the guide that the document does not have.
  • Out of scope Outside the scope of this guide.

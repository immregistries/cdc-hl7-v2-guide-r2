Technical Advisory Bulletin · January 2024

System Entry Date/Time (RXA-22) Definition

For administered and historical immunizations, RXA-22 is the most recent date and time the immunization was entered or updated in the submitting system.

Published PDF · Copy in this repository

Summary

This TAB defines RXA-22 for administered and historical immunizations as the most recent date and time the immunization was entered or updated in the submitting system, and keeps the field optional. The guide does not mention RXA-22 at all, so the definition is missing. IGAMT has RXA-22 as optional (O) in every RXA, which matches, but gives no definition.

Executive Summary; Table 1: RXA-22 definition p. 1-2

For administered and historical immunizations, RXA-22 is the most recent date and time the immunization information was entered or updated in the submitting system. This replaces the base standard's reading (the date and time it was first entered), which some IIS do not follow because they use RXA-22 for matching and deduplication.		Missing Warning

RXA-22 is not mentioned anywhere in the guide, and IGAMT has no definition for it. A submitter reading the HL7 base standard would send the first entry date, and an update would carry the same value as the original add.

Recommendation: Add the TAB's definition to 4.6 (Immunization Information), for example in 4.6.4 next to updates: "For administered and historical immunizations, RXA-22 is the most recent date and time the immunization information was entered or updated in the submitting system." List it in 5.11.3 (Reinterpretation of Base Standard Semantics), since it differs from the base definition.

Clarifications and Conformance Expectations p. 2

MAY RXA-22 remains optional. Submitting systems MAY send it, and receiving systems MAY process it.		Covered

IGAMT has RXA-22 as O in every RXA flavor (RXA_IZ01 and its administered, historical, refusal, contraindication, and not-vaccinated slices) and in RXA_IZ02 and RXA_IZ03. The narrative has no statement, but the usage matches.

4.6.4 Updates and Deletes

Out of scope p. 1-2

The meaning of RXA-22 in an RXA for a refusal, a contraindication, or a patient-level observation, and whether and how IIS use the value (for example in deduplication), are out of scope.		Covered

The guide also leaves vaccination-level deduplication to IIS, with a pointer to MIROW.

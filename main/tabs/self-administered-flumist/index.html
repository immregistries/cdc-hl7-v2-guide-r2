Technical Advisory Bulletin · September 2026

Submission of Self-Administered FluMist Vaccine to IIS

Expected VXU content for self- or caregiver-administered FluMist reported by the Asembia Specialty Pharmacy Network through STChealth. Specific to one submitter and season, so most of it is outside the scope of this guide.

Published PDF · Copy in this repository

Guide sections it affects

  • 4.6.1 Administered versus Historical Vaccinations
  • 4.6.1.1 Administered Vaccinations
  • 2.7.3 Responsible Organization (MSH-22)
  • 2.7.5 Administering Organization (RXA-11)

Summary

An interface specification for one submitter and one product, so most of it stays out of the guide. Its field conventions fit the guide and IGAMT: the identifiers assigned by the IIS, MSH-22 for the responsible pharmacy network, RXA-10 with identifier type 'MR', 'CP' only, the three VIS OBX segments, and funding with PHC70 and V01. One general point is missing from the guide: how to report a vaccine administered by the patient or a caregiver. The guide says administered ('00') means a dose "given directly by the reporting organization", while the TAB reports these doses as '00' with the patient or caregiver in RXA-10. The TAB also points to Release 1.5 for every field it does not list.

The TAB saysResultIn the guide

Background; RXA-9 and RXA-10 p. 2, 5

Self- or caregiver-administered doses are reported with RXA-9 '00' (administered, new record). RXA-10 is the patient if self-administered (echoing PID-3) or the caregiver, with identifier type 'MR'. RXA-3 is the date reported by the patient or caregiver.		Missing Warning

The guide does not mention self- or caregiver-administered vaccines. Its rule that "Administered vaccinations SHALL represent doses given directly by the reporting organization" does not fit a dose the pharmacy dispensed and the patient gave, and a reader could conclude these doses must be reported as historical. IGAMT accepts the TAB's values (RXA-10 is RE with XCN_IZ01; 'MR' is R in HL70203_IZ01).

Recommendation: Decide how self- and caregiver-administered doses are reported in general, then add it to 4.6.1: for example, "A dose dispensed by the reporting organization and administered by the patient or a caregiver is reported as administered (RXA-9 '00'), with the patient or caregiver in RXA-10." Note that CVX 333 already identifies the self- or caregiver-administered product.

4.5.3 Address Handling, 2.2.3 Notes on Data Intermediaries

PID segment; NK1 segment p. 4

Race (PID-10) and ethnic group (PID-22) use CDCREC values or 'UNK'. The phone number is sent as ^PRN^PH^^^areacode^localnumber. NK1 is sent for patients under 19.		Covered

Consistent with the Phone and Email and Unknown Race and Ethnicity TABs. 'UNK' with coding system CDCREC has the same IGAMT difference as tab-re-03 (IGAMT lists UNK under NULLFL).

4.5.5.2 Unknown and Refused Values, 4.5.4.1 General Principles, 4.5.4.2 Telecommunication Use Codes (HL70201)

ORC segment; RXA-5 to RXA-21; RXR segment p. 4-5

ORC-3 is unique in the pharmacy system; ORC-10 is empty; ORC-12 is the pharmacist who confirmed eligibility, with an NPI. RXA-5 is CVX 333 and/or the NDC unit of sale, as the jurisdiction prefers; 0.2 mL; lot, expiration, and manufacturer MED; RXA-20 'CP' only; RXA-21 'A', 'U', or 'D'. RXR-1 is nasal (C38284, NCIT); RXR-2 is empty.		Covered

These follow the guide. RXR-2 is RE in IGAMT, so leaving it empty for a nasal spray conforms. The TAB's sentence on CVX and NDC "as per jurisdictional preferences" matches the guide's coding considerations.

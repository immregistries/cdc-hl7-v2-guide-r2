2.1.5 Immunization-Specific Features

Immunization messages have unique characteristics compared to general HL7 v2 messages:

Use of RXA: In immunization messaging, the RXA segment describes vaccines that have already been administered, not just ordered.

Inclusion of VIS Data: HL7 v2 supports reporting Vaccine Information Statements (VIS), including edition dates and presentation details.

Eligibility and Funding: Messages may indicate the patient's eligibility for publicly funded vaccines (e.g., VFC).

Special Observations: OBX segments can report adverse reactions, immunity status, contraindications, and risk factors.

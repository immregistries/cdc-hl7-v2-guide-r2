4.6.4.3 IIS Matching and Correction Processing

Quality review 1 warning · 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning Statement s2 Statement combines SHALL, SHALL NOT. Each statement should carry one requirement so it can be tested and referenced on its own.qf-multi-verb-statement-04a919d8
  • Note MIROW guide Vaccination Level Deduplication in Immunization Information Systems (Introduction (scope)): Reworded. The guide cites this MIROW guide correctly in IIS Matching and Correction Processing, but its reference list ([10]) describes it as guidance on "patient and vaccination record deduplication". Patient deduplication is covered by CDC's Patient-Level De-duplication Best Practices (2013), which the guide does not cite.
    RewordedAction: Narrative
    Best practice The guide covers deduplication of vaccination events only. Patient-level deduplication (matching patient records) is out of its scope.
    Recommendation Correct the description of reference [10] to vaccination-level deduplication, and cite CDC's Patient-Level De-duplication Best Practices in Record Consolidation and Deduplication.

    See the MIROW comparison

    qf-mirow-vd-01

RXA-21 expresses the submitting system’s intended operation but does not by itself establish whether a vaccination event is new or already represented in the IIS. An add can duplicate an existing event, and an update can fail to match the earlier report. IIS matching, deduplication, and consolidation therefore remain necessary for records sent as either A or U.

The combination of submitting source and ORC-3 provides strong evidence of source-record identity when the association is reliably maintained. It supplements rather than replaces patient matching and vaccination-level matching. An IIS can use RXA-21 to understand submitter intent without treating every A as automatically new or every U as automatically matched.

Additional guidance for vaccination-level matching, deduplication, and consolidation is provided in MIROW Vaccination Level Deduplication in Immunization Information Systems [10].

Conformance statements

  1. SHOULDIIS SHOULD use the combination of submitting source and ORC-3 as a high-confidence input when matching updates and deletes to previously submitted source records.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL perform vaccination-level matching for records sent as A or U and SHALL NOT create a duplicate vaccination event solely because a correction was sent as A rather than U.⚑s2

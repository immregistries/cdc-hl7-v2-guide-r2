6.1.10 Avoiding Arbitrary Enforcement

Strictly enforcing specific aspects of the standard without considering the broader context can create significant problems, including:

Loss of Data: Rejecting data unnecessarily harms data quality and undermines public health objectives.

Increased Costs: Rigid enforcement can impose unnecessary burdens on partners, reducing overall system efficiency.

Barriers to Collaboration: Overly strict requirements can damage trust and collaboration between systems.

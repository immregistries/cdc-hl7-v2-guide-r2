In some situations, a submitting system may hold immunization data for patients that has not previously been shared with the IIS. This most commonly occurs during initial onboarding of a clinic or system that is connecting to the IIS for the first time, but may also arise when a system is replaced, reconfigured, or when significant operational issues raise concern about data completeness.

In these cases, it may be appropriate for the submitting system to transmit a patient's complete immunization history to the IIS as part of a coordinated initial data load. This one-time or limited-duration submission helps establish a complete baseline record in the IIS and ensures that historical vaccinations are available for forecasting, coverage assessment, and patient matching. IIS are encouraged to discuss the need for such submissions during onboarding and to define expectations for scope, timing, and review.

Because bulk or complete-history submissions can have operational and data-quality implications, they must be coordinated in advance with the IIS. After this initial synchronization is complete, routine VXU submissions reflecting new or changed immunization events are generally sufficient to keep the IIS record up to date.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYA submitting system MAY trigger a VXU containing the patient's complete immunization history upon first submission.s1
  2. SHALLThe submitting system SHALL coordinate with the IIS prior to such submissions.s2

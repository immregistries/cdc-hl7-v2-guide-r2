Claiming conformance to the National IG is a representation to partners and the broader community, and it carries expectations of reusability and alignment with nationally agreed behavior. Jurisdictions making such claims must ensure that consumers can discover how the claim was determined and where to find any local differences.

  • "Conformant with the National IG": used when the Local IG is compliant with National IG requirements (differences, if any, are constraints that preserve compliance).

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLA jurisdiction SHALL claim conformance to the National IG only if its Local IG does not contradict National IG requirements.s1
  2. SHOULDJurisdictions SHOULD distinguish clearly between these statements when publishing local guidance:s2
  3. SHALL"Based on the National IG with jurisdiction-specific differences": used when the Local IG includes documented differences, whether constraints or contradictions. When using this label, the jurisdiction SHALL indicate for each difference whether it is a compliant constraint or a contradiction that affects interoperability.s3
  4. SHALL NOTJurisdictions SHALL NOT imply national endorsement or standard compliance when local requirements require non-standard behavior from submitting systems.s4

TABs are critical tools for implementers and have specific usage requirements:

Optional Functionality: Some TABs offer optional functionalities or capabilities that organizations MAY choose to support based on their specific needs.

Guidance for Development: TABs provide implementers with the clarity needed to maintain and enhance their systems' interoperability.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLRequired Implementation: Organizations SHALL implement TAB specifications as soon as feasible to maintain HL7 v2.5.1 exchange capabilities.s1

4.5.1.5 Identifier Changes, Merges, and Retirement

Quality review 1 warning

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning MIROW guide Consolidating Demographic Records and Vaccination Event Records (BR202, BR203 - Retain all past IIS patient IDs; use the current one): Partly covered. The guide requires the current SR or LR in every RSP (as BR203 asks), but says retired identifiers "may be retained as aliases, but a complete identifier history is not required". Submitters are told they can store the IIS identifier (from code 5044 or an RSP) and use it in later queries; after a merge, the one they hold may be retired. The guide does not say whether a query naming a retired SR or LR finds the merged record.
    Partly coveredAction: CDC decision
    Best practice All past IIS patient IDs associated with a consolidated record should be retained. The current IIS patient ID should be used in all IIS-originated communications, and in response to a query the IIS should let the submitter know that the IIS patient ID has changed.
    Recommendation CDC to decide whether an IIS SHOULD resolve a retired SR or LR in QPD-3 to the current record (which MIROW BR202 makes possible), and to say so in Identifier Changes, Merges, and Retirement.

    See the MIROW comparison

    qf-mirow-cr-03

A primary exchange identifier can change after a merge, correction, or system conversion. The IIS may therefore receive a new identifier for a patient previously reported by the same submitter. The IIS associates the new identifier through patient matching, and it becomes current for later exchange.

An SR or LR can also change when IIS patient records are merged. The IIS may retain one existing identifier, assign a new one, and retire others. Historical identifiers may be retained as aliases, but a complete identifier history is not required.

An identifier can be replaced or retired, but it cannot be recycled for another person. Retaining an identifier after records for the same person are merged is not reuse.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTSending systems SHALL NOT reassign a previously used primary exchange identifier to a different patient.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL support receiving a new primary exchange identifier for a patient previously reported by the same submitting system.s2

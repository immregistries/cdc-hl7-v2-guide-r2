4.5.1.5 Identifier Changes, Merges, and Retirement

A primary exchange identifier can change after a merge, correction, or system conversion. The IIS may therefore receive a new identifier for a patient previously reported by the same submitter. The IIS associates the new identifier through patient matching, and it becomes current for later exchange.

An SR or LR can also change when IIS patient records are merged. The IIS may retain one existing identifier, assign a new one, and retire others. Historical identifiers may be retained as aliases, but a complete identifier history is not required.

An identifier can be replaced or retired, but it cannot be recycled for another person. Retaining an identifier after records for the same person are merged is not reuse.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTSending systems SHALL NOT reassign a previously used primary exchange identifier to a different patient.s1
  2. SHALLIIS SHALL support receiving a new primary exchange identifier for a patient previously reported by the same submitting system.s2

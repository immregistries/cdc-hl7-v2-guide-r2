All forecasts are organized under a single ORC/RXA segment pair so the entire recommendation set is logically grouped and cannot be fragmented across multiple order groups. Individual antigen forecasts within that group are distinguished using OBX-4 sub-ID values; a forecast must be returned for every age-based series, even when no further action is required. Earlier practices of returning only due series, or suppressing complete or aged-out series, are no longer sufficient for interoperability.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLAll forecast information SHALL be returned under a single ORC/RXA order group.⚑s1
  2. SHALLEach antigen's forecast SHALL be distinguished using OBX-4 sub-ID values.⚑s2
  3. SHALLA forecast SHALL be included for every standard age-based series, including recommended series (for example, on-schedule or not yet due to start) and not-recommended series (for example, complete, immune, or contraindicated).⚑s3

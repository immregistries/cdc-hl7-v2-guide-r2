6.1.6 Responsibility of Systems to Enforce Standards

Systems operating under the latest HL7 v2.5.1 Implementation Guide for Immunization Messaging are not responsible for enforcing compliance with the standard among their partners. Instead, their primary focus should remain on supporting data quality, interoperability, and program goals. Strict enforcement of every aspect of the standard, especially in a way that leads to loss of valuable data, contradicts these objectives.

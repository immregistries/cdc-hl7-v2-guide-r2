4.8.8.4 Clinical Interpretation and Validation

This guide treats any reported history of disease as a clinician-validated diagnosis. There is no distinction between suspected and confirmed disease, and no minimum validation criteria are defined. The purpose of these observations is to convey documented clinical knowledge, not to adjudicate diagnostic accuracy. Serological results may provide stronger evidence than a recorded history of disease or patient-reported history, but they are treated equivalently only in terms of message acceptance, not in clinical interpretation or forecasting.

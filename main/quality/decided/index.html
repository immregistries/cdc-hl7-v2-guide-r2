Deferred and waived findings

A quality finding can be closed without a change to the text in two ways. It can be waived, when the text is intended as written, or deferred, when it will be fixed in the next version. Either way it no longer counts as open, so the open counts on the Quality review can reach zero. Each one stays listed here with its reason.

To ask for a finding to be waived or deferred, comment on the finding (the Comment link under it) and say which and why. The authors record the decision in quality/dispositions.yml, and it stays attached to the finding from one build to the next.

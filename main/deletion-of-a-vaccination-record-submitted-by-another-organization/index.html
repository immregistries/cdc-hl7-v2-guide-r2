3.2.5.5 Deletion of a Vaccination Record Submitted by Another Organization

Submitting systems may encounter vaccination records that they did not originate, often as a result of consolidation performed by the IIS across multiple reporting sources. In these situations, the submitting system does not have sufficient context to safely determine whether a vaccination should be removed from the IIS record.

Deletion of vaccinations reported by another organization is fundamentally a record-cleanup activity that cannot be reliably or safely handled through HL7 v2 VXU messaging alone. IIS are not required to honor delete requests for vaccinations submitted by other organizations, and in many cases should not, as doing so risks removing valid immunization events supported by independent reporting sources.

This scenario commonly arises when submitting systems identify what appear to be duplicate vaccinations after receiving consolidated data from the IIS. In these cases, the duplication may already have been resolved or intentionally retained by the IIS based on its internal reconciliation logic. Attempting to delete such records via VXU can result in conflicting actions that do not reflect the IIS's full view of the patient record.

When a submitting system believes that duplicate or incorrect vaccinations originating from other organizations exist in the IIS, the appropriate course of action is to work directly with the IIS through established data-quality or support processes. This ensures that cleanup decisions are made with full visibility into all contributing sources and that the patient's immunization history remains accurate and complete.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTThe submitting system SHALL NOT trigger a VXU when deleting a vaccination originally submitted by another organization.s1
  2. SHALLThe submitting system SHALL coordinate directly with the IIS to request record cleanup.s2
  3. MAYIIS MAY refuse delete requests for vaccinations submitted by other organizations.s3

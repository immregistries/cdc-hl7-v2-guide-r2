4.6.2.8 Other Uses of CVX Codes

In addition to representing administered and historical vaccinations, CVX is reused throughout immunization messaging to identify vaccine concepts in related clinical and public health contexts. These uses rely on the ability of CVX to consistently represent vaccine types and antigens, even when no specific product or administration event is being reported.

One important secondary use of CVX is in the communication of immunization evaluation and recommendation (forecasting) results generated by clinical decision support systems. In these contexts, CVX codes are used to represent the vaccine type or antigen being evaluated or recommended, rather than a specific administered product. The set of CVX codes used for evaluation and forecasting is therefore a subset of the full CVX code set and is largely composed of unspecified or category-level CVX codes. Detailed structural and semantic requirements for CDS-related messaging are defined in Section 4.7, Clinical Decision Support.

CVX is also used to associate other immunization-related information with a vaccine concept, including Vaccine Information Statements (VIS), refusals, and contraindications. For refusals and contraindications, CVX identifies the vaccination that was not given and functions similarly to administered vaccination reporting, minus the administration event. VIS association and forecasting, by contrast, are derived uses that leverage CVX as a stable way to reference vaccine concepts across systems.

These uses do not change the canonical role of CVX in representing vaccinations but reflect its broader utility as a shared vocabulary for immunization-related concepts.

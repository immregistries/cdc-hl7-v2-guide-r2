PD1-12 communicates the patient's explicit consent preference regarding data sharing. It must not be used to reflect jurisdiction-wide defaults or assumed values.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSystems SHALL use PD1-12 (Protection Indicator) only to communicate explicit patient or guardian consent preferences.s1
  2. SHALL'Y' SHALL indicate that the patient/guardian has not consented to data sharing (protect data).⚑s2
  3. SHALL'N' SHALL indicate that the patient/guardian has consented to data sharing.⚑s3
  4. SHALLAn empty PD1-12 SHALL indicate that no determination of patient/guardian preference is available.⚑s4
  5. SHALL NOTSending systems SHALL NOT populate PD1-12 by default unless the patient/guardian has made an explicit determination.s5
  6. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT require sending systems to populate PD1-12 with a defaults6
  7. SHOULDIIS SHOULD apply jurisdiction-specific defaults or restrictions after message receipt.s7

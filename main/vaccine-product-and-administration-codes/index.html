4.6.2 Vaccine Product and Administration Codes

Accurate vaccine coding is foundational to interoperable immunization data exchange. IIS depend on consistent vaccine identifiers to consolidate records from multiple sources, support clinical decision support, enable forecasting and evaluation, and return interpretable histories across jurisdictions and over time. Poorly defined or inconsistently applied coding rules lead to fragmented histories, unreliable evaluation results, and brittle integrations that fail as products evolve.

Multiple code systems are used in immunization workflows because they serve different purposes. CVX provides a stable, clinical representation of what vaccination the patient received. NDC identifies the specific manufactured product and packaging used at the point of administration. CPT reflects billing and procedural context rather than vaccine identity. IIS must reconcile these perspectives while preserving traceability and long-term interpretability.

This section defines how vaccine product and administration codes are expected to be used, received, stored, mapped, and returned in HL7 v2 immunization messaging. The intent is not to introduce new policy decisions, but to document current best practice, clarify long-standing ambiguities, and ensure consistent behavior across submitting systems and IIS.

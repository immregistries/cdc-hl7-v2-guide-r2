3.4.5 Patient Matching Responsibilities

Accurate patient matching is a shared responsibility between the querying system and the IIS. Querying systems are responsible for providing complete and current identifying information, while IIS are responsible for applying appropriate matching logic to determine the most likely patient record or set of candidate records.

Effective matching depends on cooperation between systems over time. Information previously submitted to the IIS, particularly patient identifiers, can significantly improve match accuracy and performance when reused consistently in subsequent queries.

Querying System Responsibilities

Patient identifiers such as Medical Record Numbers (MRNs) SHALL be included when available.

IIS Responsibilities

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLQuerying systems SHALL send all known patient demographic and identifying information available at the time of query.s1
  2. SHOULDQuerying systems SHOULD send the most current patient information, even if it differs from previously submitted data.s2
  3. SHOULDWhen querying systems both submit VXU messages and send QBP queries, the same patient identifiers SHOULD be used consistently across both interactions.⚑s3
  4. SHALLState immunization registry identifiers (SR) SHALL be included when known, such as when obtained from prior candidate match responses.s4
  5. SHALLIIS SHALL use all patient data submitted in the query to identify the most likely matching record or records.s5
  6. SHOULDIIS SHOULD use probabilistic, weighted, machine learning, or other advanced matching techniques rather than relying solely on exact field matches.s6
  7. SHOULDIIS SHOULD retain and reuse patient identifiers received through prior VXU submissions when those identifiers are consistent with other key patient attributes.s7
  8. SHOULDIIS SHOULD treat strong patient identifiers (such as MRNs) as high-confidence match indicators while validating consistency with other demographic data.s8
  9. SHOULDIIS SHOULD document how patient identifiers are used operationally to support matching and performance.s9

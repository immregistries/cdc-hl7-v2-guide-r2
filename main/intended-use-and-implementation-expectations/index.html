The CDS information in an RSP is designed for clinical use. It provides complete, unsuppressed outputs that downstream systems may present, filter, or suppress according to clinical context. Because clinical recommendations evolve, registries must maintain CDS engines capable of reflecting the most current immunization recommendations.

The CDC Clinical Decision Support for Immunization (CDSi) project is the primary CDC resource for implementers seeking computable alignment with national ACIP/CDC immunization recommendations; implementers should also account for other applicable schedule authorities where jurisdictional policy differs. IIS implementers are expected to monitor immunization recommendation updates, incorporate revised logic into their CDS engines, and perform regular testing. CDSi-provided supporting data and test cases are the recommended primary resources for national ACIP/CDC alignment and should be used where applicable, supplemented with locally developed scenarios to address jurisdictional policies or additional schedule authorities. Supporting these practices enables registries to produce consistent and interpretable CDS output and to participate effectively in interoperability measurement programs.

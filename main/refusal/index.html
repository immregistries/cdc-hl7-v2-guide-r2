4.8.7 Refusal

Many factors can explain why a vaccination was not administered at a visit: the provider may have missed the dose, the vaccine was unavailable, it was not yet due, the clinician and patient made a shared decision not to vaccinate, the patient missed the appointment, or the patient had a temporary contraindication.

Refusal is a distinct and specific category. It occurs only when a vaccination was due (on or after its recommendation date), the clinician would have administered it, and the patient or guardian declined.

The purpose of recording refusals is to measure non-compliance due to patient or guardian choice, not to track every reason a vaccine was not given. These data are valuable for monitoring public health trends and vaccine hesitancy but should not alter clinical decision support, forecasting, or patient care. Patients who have refused remain due for vaccination unless medically contraindicated.

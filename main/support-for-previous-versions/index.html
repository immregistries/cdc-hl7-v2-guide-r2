6.1.2 Support for Previous Versions

While systems are encouraged to upgrade promptly, continued support for the immediate previous version of the guide (published in 2018) is essential. Systems should strive to encourage their partners to upgrade but should avoid refusing data or enforcing upgrades solely for perceived compliance purposes. The overarching goal remains the exchange of high-quality immunization data, and adherence to the guide serves as a means to achieve this goal.

Systems relying on older versions, such as HL7 v2.3.1 or v2.4, are strongly encouraged to upgrade as soon as possible. Neither v2.3.1 nor v2.4 are supported nationally, and it is acceptable for systems to require partners to adopt the latest HL7 v2.5.1 standards. Transitioning away from these outdated versions ensures better alignment with current community-supported standards and practices.

Policy requirements and HL7 v2 conformance requirements are not the same thing, though they are often conflated in practice: policy says what must be collected, while HL7 usage codes say what must appear in the message. IIS implementers routinely blur these two concepts, often under pressure from state or federal program staff who use the same word, "required," for both. This leads to misinterpretation, misconfiguration, and ultimately worse data. The purpose of this section is to clarify how IIS should translate programmatic requirements into HL7 v2 conformance statements correctly and consistently, in a way that supports policy goals rather than undermining them.

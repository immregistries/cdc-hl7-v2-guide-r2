4.5.7.4 IIS-Derived Status and Jurisdictional Views

Quality review 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note 1 requirement was written as a paragraph in the draft and is shown here as conformance statement (wording unchanged): s1.qf-statement-from-paragraph-463fd456

IIS may maintain internal status classifications that reflect jurisdictional considerations, such as inferred inactivity due to lack of recent vaccination activity or confirmed death from vital records. These statuses are derived internally and are not part of HL7 v2 immunization messaging.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTJurisdictional or IIS-derived patient statuses SHALL NOT be transmitted in HL7 v2 messages.s1

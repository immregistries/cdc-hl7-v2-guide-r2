4.5.7.4 IIS-Derived Status and Jurisdictional Views

Quality review 2 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors. Not part of the guide.

  • Note Statement s1 MIROW guide Management of Patient Status in Immunization Information Systems (P305 - Make status changes available to providers): Partly covered. The guide forbids transmitting jurisdictional or IIS-derived statuses in HL7 v2 messages, which is right for the jurisdiction level but leaves no message path for P305 at the provider level (for example, an IIS marking a patient inactive for a provider after a death confirmed by Vital Records). P305 can be met through reports or the user interface, but the guide does not say so.
    Partly coveredAction: Narrative
    Best practice The IIS should make available to a provider organization the changes it makes to the status of a patient associated with that provider organization.
    Recommendation Add to IIS-Derived Status and Jurisdictional Views that IIS share provider-level status changes they make by other means, such as reports or the user interface (MIROW P305).

    See the MIROW comparison

  • Note 1 requirement was written as a paragraph in the draft and is shown here as conformance statement (wording unchanged): s1.qf-statement-from-paragraph-463fd456

IIS may maintain internal status classifications that reflect jurisdictional considerations, such as inferred inactivity due to lack of recent vaccination activity or confirmed death from vital records. These statuses are derived internally and are not part of HL7 v2 immunization messaging.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALL NOTJurisdictional or IIS-derived patient statuses SHALL NOT be transmitted in HL7 v2 messages.⚑s1

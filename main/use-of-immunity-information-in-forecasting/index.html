4.8.8.2 Use of Immunity Information in Forecasting

Quality review 1 warning · 1 note

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Warning Statement s3 TAB Returning IIS Patient Immunization Record Information in a Z32 and Z42 RSP (Table 1: Observations associated with a patient): Partly covered. The guide says IIS that use immunity information for recommendations SHOULD return it, which is narrower than the TAB, and has no statement about returning contraindications. It makes returning patient-level reactions a SHOULD where the TAB says MAY.
    Partly coveredAction: Narrative
    TAB Contraindications (vaccine-specific, and not vaccine-specific with product '998'), disease with serological evidence of immunity (75505-8), and disease with presumed immunity (59784-9) SHOULD be returned. An adverse reaction not tied to an administration MAY be returned.
    Recommendation Add the TAB's SHOULD for contraindications and both immunity observations to 3.5.2 or 4.8.

    See the TAB comparison

    qf-tab-rr-06
  • Note Statement s3 It does not open with the system or party it applies to, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-83b4e7fd

Immunity information does not automatically modify vaccination recommendations. Whether immunity affects evaluation or future dose requirements depends on local policy and, importantly, on whether and how that policy has been operationalized in the CDS logic implemented by the IIS or its forecasting partner. IIS that store immunity information may or may not use it when generating recommendations; two jurisdictions may have the same policy but differ in whether and how they have implemented that policy in their CDS engines.

Conformance statements

  1. MAYIIS MAY use immunity information when generating recommendations.s1
  2. SHALL NOTSubmitting systems SHALL NOT assume immunity information will affect recommendations.s2
  3. SHOULDIIS that use immunity information for recommendations SHOULD return the relevant observations in the response to maintain transparency.⚑s3
  4. SHALLIIS SHALL accept immunity information even if they do not currently use it for recommendations.s4

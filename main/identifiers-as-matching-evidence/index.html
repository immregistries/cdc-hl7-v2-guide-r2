4.5.1.6 Identifiers as Matching Evidence

A known primary exchange identifier provides a fast lookup and a strong match signal, but it is not proof. An identifier can be entered incorrectly, attached to the wrong record, or improperly reused.

A new identifier does not establish continuity with an existing IIS record. The IIS uses demographic and other identifying information to associate it with the patient. A known identifier with materially conflicting demographics may indicate a collision or incorrect association and should not result in an automatic update.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLIIS SHALL evaluate demographic information before associating a new primary exchange identifier with an existing patient record.s1
  2. SHALL NOTIIS SHALL NOT rely solely on a primary exchange identifier when demographics materially conflict with the associated patient record.s2

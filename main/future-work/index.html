4.7.7.1 Future work

Quality review 2 notes

Findings from automated and editorial checks, for the authors and reviewers. Not part of the guide.

  • Note Another card has the same title (4.7.7.3 Future work). Distinct titles make navigation and links clearer.qf-duplicate-title-c366b9f1
  • Note Statement s2 Its subject is "Dose-validity OBX for that evaluation instance", not a system or party, so it is unclear who must comply. Naming the responsible party (for example, "Submitting systems SHALL …" or "IIS SHALL …") makes the requirement testable.qf-no-actor-1afa08ef

Specific areas where further guidance may be developed include requiring a standardized "invalid dose reason" code when dose validity = N, standardizing the coding and counting rules for dose numbers and total doses in a series to reduce inter-engine variability, and expanding or refining dose-validity code sets beyond the current Y/N model to capture additional, interoperable semantics.

Conformance statements

  1. SHOULDImplementers SHOULD document CVX support and local mappings when Unspecified Formulation values are used.s1
  2. SHALLIf dose validity cannot be determined or is unavailable at query time, the dose-validity OBX for that evaluation instance SHALL be omitted.⚑s2

Funding eligibility indicates whether the patient was eligible for a publicly funded vaccination program for a specific administered dose. Although some funding programs apply eligibility rules that are effectively patient-level (for example, all VFC-eligible vaccines at an encounter), implementations must record and message eligibility at the vaccination level to support accurate program reporting and MIROW-aligned practices.

Legacy workflows may capture eligibility only at the visit-level. The OBX-17 observation method allows systems and IIS to distinguish per-visit from per-dose capture and to troubleshoot older interfaces, but current implementations are expected to support per-dose reporting.

See Chapter 7, Message Infrastructure, and the OBX co-constraint tables, Section 7.2.18, for the normative HL7/LOINC field-level specifications, including OBX-3, OBX-5, and OBX-17 usage and exact code bindings.

Conformance statements

  1. SHALLSubmitting systems SHALL support capture and messaging of funding eligibility at the vaccination (per-dose) level.s1
  2. SHALLIf a submitting system only captures eligibility at the visit-level, it SHALL associate that eligibility with each administered vaccination from that visit when constructing the message.⚑s2
  3. SHALLFor each administered vaccination, at most one funding eligibility value SHALL be sent.⚑s3
  4. MAYIIS MAY use OBX-17 to diagnose legacy or misconfigured interfaces but are not required to use OBX-17 to drive program logic.s4

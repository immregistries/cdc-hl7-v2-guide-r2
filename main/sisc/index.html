SISC Guidance

Before the TAB series began in 2024, AIRA's Standards and Interoperability Steering Committee (SISC) published guidance documents, with CDC, that clarified how to implement Release 1.5. Implementers built to them, and some TABs build on them. These pages check, requirement by requirement, whether this guide folds each one in, so that nothing implementers rely on is dropped by accident.

Two related documents are compared elsewhere. The 2023 draft guidance on a national set of error codes became part of the ACK TAB; the 2017 version listed here is compared only for what the TAB did not carry over. The 2015 MIROW micro-guide on validating lot numbers was replaced by the unit of sale and unit of use lot number guidance listed here. A few related documents published by AIRA or the Immunization Integration Program, rather than SISC, are listed here too; each one's page says so.

Each document links to its published copy on the AIRA repository and to the copy kept with this guide's source. 7 of 24 documents have been compared so far. Results that need attention also appear as quality findings on the guide sections they concern.

What the results mean

  • Conflict The guide or the profiles say something different.
  • Missing Not in the guide.
  • Partly covered Some of it is in the guide.
  • Reworded The guide says it differently; check that the meaning is unchanged.
  • In narrative only The guide says it, but in narrative rather than as a numbered conformance statement.
  • Covered The guide says the same thing.
  • Guide adds A requirement in the guide that the document does not have.
  • Out of scope Outside the scope of this guide.

System Entry Date/Time (RXA-22)
Definition

Executive Summary

A definition of the optional RXA-22 System Entry Date/Time field is provided for when an
RXA is used to message an administered or historical immunization(s). In these instances,
RXA-22 is defined as the most recent date/time when the administered or historical
immunization information was last entered/updated in the submitting system.

Background

In the National IG, RXA-22 System Entry Date/Time is an optional field with no definition
provided. The RXA segment is used in a VXU message to convey immunization information
associated with a patient.
Two competing definitions have emerged for the RXA-22 field:

    • The initial date/time of entry in the submitting system
    • The most recent date/time of entry in the submitting system (i.e., potentially the

         date/time of an update)

The HL7 2.5.1 base standard, from which the National IG was derived, defines RXA-22 as
“[d]ate/time the administration information was entered into the source system.” This may
imply that the value represents the initial date/time of entry and that perhaps it is not
expected to be changed when updates are made.

However, some immunization information systems (IIS) interpret this as the most recent
date/time of entry, which may represent the initial date/time of entry or the date/time the
entry in the submitting system was updated. These IIS use the value in RXA-22 to support
matching and deduplication of submitted immunization information.

Scope

In scope
A definition for the RXA-22 System Entry Date/Time field as it relates to messaging an
administered or historical immunization.
Out of scope

    • Definition of RXA-22 as it relates to an RXA conveying a vaccine refusal, a specific
         vaccine contraindication, or a patient-level observation.

    • Usage of the RXA-22 field.

Clarifications and Conformance Expectations

RXA System Entry Date/Time remains an optional field, i.e.:

• Submitting systems MAY choose to send RXA-22.
• Receiving systems MAY choose to process RXA-22.
